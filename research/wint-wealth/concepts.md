# Wint Wealth: concepts

30 ads (19 video, 11 image) fall into 22 concepts. Counts are ads in ads.json whose `video_asset` equals the asset id (image ads count 1 each). A concept's count is the sum over its assets, and the counts add up to 30, the number of ads in ads.json. Rules: same footage or same image layout in another shape, length, end card or with a different bond named on the card is one concept; the same script performed by a different person is a new concept (linked by `script_twin`). The only script twin in the brand is c02 (1314615330761972 and 1551790489702609).

## Series and recurring devices

- **"Only 10% of Indians know bonds"** opener or early line, usually with the caption "As per the SEBI Investor Survey 2025": c04 (1462175878537057), c09 (2069991826945564), c10 (2378355306351298), c11 (2553317288476600, spoken, no source). Nine ads in all (c04 1, c09 1, c10 6, c11 1).
- **Second source of income** as the promise: c01, c05, c09, c10, c11 (11 ads: 1 + 2 + 1 + 6 + 1).
- **Couples**: c06, c07, c11, all unlabelled people; c06 and c07 are acted, c11 is to camera.
- **Family lines**: c03 ("mera beta toh meri baat nahi sunta") and c12 (a mother who saved her whole life).
- **Sell any time or easy exit**: c03, c05, c06, c07, c09 (with a caption that a sale depends on a buyer), c12 (app screen "Sell anytime").
- **Skin in the game**: c01 (spoken) and c11 (spoken, "2%").
- **Same footage, different shape or length**: c02 (44.7 s and 23.3 s, landscape 3:2); c16 (square and 9:16); c05 runs as one asset in a Play Store ad and an App Store ad; c10 runs as six ads, one with an App Store link.
- **Paper-craft and hand-drawn explainers**: c02 (stop-motion), c04 (hand-drawn paper cut with a talking head).
- **Creator or collaboration credit**: c01 ("Brand Collaboration, Finance Paulified, Anuj Paul"), c08 ("#ad", "My First Paid Post"); c04's ad text says "Check the link from my bio!" with no name.
- **Shared end card on every video**: "EARN 11% FIXED RETURNS (P.A.)", "3 Month Tenure Bond", card "11% p.a. / Maturity: 3 months / Navi Bond | A rating | Senior Secured Bond", plus the same disclaimer ("9-12% fixed returns per annum"). Every static carries the same Navi Bond foot line.
- **SEBI registered-name strip** (Fourdegreewater Services Private Limited, INZ000313632) along the videos and a "SEBI REGISTERED BROKER" logo on every static.
- **Monthly interest and calendar motif**: nine of the eleven static ads (all except c14 and c20) say "Monthly Interest" in the main text; c18, c19 and c20 add a calendar, c20 reading "Earn Regular Income".
- **Dynamic creative**: all 11 statics are multi-card dynamic creative (3 or 4 cards, only the first card tagged), as are the video ad 2292299894864285 (c12, 3 cards). c19 (1819131125779645) shows the unfilled placeholders `{{product.name}}` and `{{product.brand}}`; it is tagged from the creative alone with `ad_text_missing`.
- **Shared ad copy**: "Start your monthly interest today with bonds. No lock-in. Invest now." with headline "3L+ trusted investors." runs on 10 ads (statics c13, c14, c16 x2, c17, c18, c20, c22, plus videos c03 and c12). "Invest in secured bonds. Fixed monthly returns." runs on c15 and c21. The six c10 ads and the two c05 ads each share their own text.

## Numbers that disagree across ads

- **Tenure (3 vs 6 vs 18 vs 20 months)**: every video end card and nine statics say "3 months" or "3 Month Tenure Bond"; 2069991826945564 says "teen mahine ke pakke short-term bonds". Against that, static 1375574504560912 shows "Maturity: 6 months" on the card while its print says "12 months tenure" for "U GRO Jun'25"; static 1819131125779645 says "6 months tenure" (its sister cards say 3); 1462175878537057 speaks of "3 se 6 mahine ke short term options" while its app list shows Navi Finserv with "Maturity left 20 months" (and others at 25 and 15-29 months); 1582906947180001's app list shows 11% at 20 months (Navi Finserv), 11.75% at 24 months, 9% at 55 days and 8.25% at 4 months; 28008431572111270's app screen shows Muthoot MCred "Maturity left 18 months" with "Interest Quarterly" against its own 3 month end card. 1597073601774076 speaks of a "6 mahine ka fund" but sells a 3 month card.
- **Monthly payout vs 3 month bond**: "monthly" or "har mahine" is claimed in 1582906947180001, 2553317288476600, 2378355306351298 (monthly, quarterly or yearly), and on nine static ads, while the card says "Maturity: 3 months" and 28008431572111270 says "Interest Quarterly".
- **Minimum investment (1,000 vs 10,000 vs 50,000)**: "sirf 1000 rupay se" is spoken in 1462175878537057, whose app list shows "Min. 10k" (Navi Finserv, Arman Financial) and "Min. 90k" (Finnable Credit). Rs 10,000 is spoken and in the ad text of 2378355306351298, and printed on statics 1078474831621182, 1375574504560912 and 955959980393839; 28008431572111270's app screen shows "You invest Rs 10,000". 1061819343233299 says the client started with 50,000 rupees.
- **Rate (11 vs 11.75 vs 9 to 12%)**: "11% fixed" on almost every ad and end card; "11.75% p.a" on static 1375574504560912 (and 11.75% at 24 months in the 1582906947180001 app list); "9 to 12%" spoken in 1061819343233299 and 1597073601774076, in the ad text of 1087294090969954 and in every disclaimer; "upto 11%" in 1365632395557182; "11% ke aaspaas" with a caption of 9% to 12% in 2069991826945564. App lists also show 11.5%, 11.25%, 9% and 8.25%. The 11% on the card is one 3 month bond, not the platform rate, and the disclaimer says fixed returns are not guaranteed.
- **Legal name**: "Fourdegreewater Services Private Limited" on the SEBI strip of the videos and on the 9:16 statics (1768821550864003 and 1819131125779645 read "FOURDEGREEWATER SERVICES PVT. LTD."), against "Wint Securities Private Limited (Formerly known as Fourdegreewater Services Private Limited)" on the end cards of 1597073601774076 and 2378355306351298 and the legal line of static 1637283661235770.
- **Investors and repayment**: "3 lakh+" investors in 1462175878537057, 2378355306351298, 2553317288476600 and the headline "3L+ trusted investors"; the headline of 1637283661235770 is "Transparent Investing Always" instead. 1087294090969954's ad text claims "1500 Cr+ repaid on time", not shown in the video.
- **Skin in the game**: 2553317288476600 says Wint puts in "2%" of its money in every bond (unclear in the transcript); 1061819343233299 says only "skin in the game" while its 55 s small print says holdings may be sold at any time.
- **Selling**: "sell at any time, no questions asked" (1365632395557182) and ad text "No lock-in" against the print and captions that a sale depends on a buyer (2069991826945564, static 1078474831621182); 1582906947180001's app bar reads "Sell now and get money by next working day".
