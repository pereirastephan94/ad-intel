# Cult.fit: concepts

30 ads (all video, no image ads) fall into 5 concepts. Counts are ads in ads.json whose `video_asset` equals the asset id. A concept's count is the sum over its assets, and the counts add up to 30, the number of ads in ads.json (8 + 9 + 4 + 8 + 1). Rules: the same footage in another shape or length, or with another price or city on the end card, is one concept. The Fitstart barbell version (c05) is new footage with a new pass, so it is its own concept, though it shares the c04 layout.

## Series and recurring devices

- **Fitstart Days sale card**: c04 and c05, "FITSTART DAYS / cultpass PLAY or ELITE starting at Rs X/month + Daily Deals", "INDIA'S BIGGEST FITNESS SALE" and a ring "OFFERS THAT ARE OUT OF THIS WORLD" round a rupee coin. Nine ads (c04 8, c05 1), all from 2026-11-16. The video says "Fitstart Days", the ad text says "Fitstart Sale".
- **One film, many centres and prices**: c01, c02 and c03 are re-sent with a new "Start your fitness journey at Cult <place>" text and, for c01 and c02, a new price on the end card. Places seen: Mall Road Amritsar (2404800830259411, 1142965181654720, 1763133728096239), Chandigarh (28835523932756470), VIP Road (1645883207287485, 1088984333744777), Prahlad Nagar (4606351752947622, 1652879219724213), Indira Nagar (1644460903932522), Indore (1114356351358840), Neo Gym (1182726674293191). The text always ends "we'll call you with all the details", so the button is a lead form (landing is only fb.me).
- **Gag template "event, question, same answer"**: c03 only, run for Indira Nagar, VIP Road, Amritsar and Neo Gym.
- **Headline set**: "Lowest Prices Today" or "Lowest Prices on cultpass" sits over c01 and c02 cards at ₹1,099, ₹1,199, ₹1,250 and ₹1,291; "Claim your FREE GYM TRIAL" over c02 (Indore) and c03.
- **Same ad text on many ads**: "Explore strength training, yoga, dance fitness and gym workouts with Cult. Plans start at ₹1,099/month." runs on 10 ads (c01 x5, c02 x5). The long cultpass PLAY copy is shared by all eight c04 ads; the c05 ad has its own Hyderabad text.
- **No person is named or labelled** in any video: the c01 and c02 woman and the c03 man are unlabelled, and no creator or founder credit appears.
- **Dynamic creative**: 1529622859191185 and its four siblings (3 cards), 4606351752947622, 1114356351358840, 1644460903932522, 1763133728096239, 1088984333744777, 1182726674293191 (4 cards) and 1950951139220699 (5 cards) are multi-card ads; only the first card is tagged.

## Numbers that disagree across ads

- **Same tour film, three prices (c02)**: end card "starting at ₹1,099/mo" on 986842284018835, 1153711787290276, 1440279738302594, 2056660201685004 and 997032080107644 (ad text says ₹1,099 too); "₹1,199/mo" on 1645883207287485 and 1652879219724213; "₹1,250/mo" on 1142965181654720; no price on 1114356351358840. The voice never says a price.
- **Same "don't go" film, four prices (c01)**: ₹1,099 on 1529622859191185 and its four siblings (no city), ₹1,199 on 4606351752947622 (Cult Gym PrahladNagar), ₹1,250 on 2404800830259411 (Amritsar), ₹1,291 on 28835523932756470 (Chandigarh). The ad text of the last two gives no price, so the card is the only figure.
- **Fitstart price**: cultpass PLAY "starting at ₹1,145/month + Exciting Daily Deals" in the vertical cut (1665600734881123, 1126343416846783, 2555329068326204) against "₹1,104/month + Daily Deals" in the square cut (2958662064496104, 1844076343579583, 3294825420716053, 1601836728299392, 2127525884795681); cultpass ELITE "₹1,162/month*" on 1950951139220699 with no footnote. The ordinary ads sell ELITE from ₹1,099 to ₹1,291, so "India's biggest fitness sale" is not cheaper than the everyday price.
- **"Lowest prices" against the price card**: headlines "Lowest Prices Today" and "Lowest Prices on cultpass" sit over ₹1,099, ₹1,199, ₹1,250 and ₹1,291 cards (986842284018835 at ₹1,099, 1645883207287485 at ₹1,199, 2404800830259411 at ₹1,250, 28835523932756470 at ₹1,291). On the c01 ads the card says "Explore cult", not cultpass. No comparison or date is shown. "Limited-time deal, sign up today" (4606351752947622, 1652879219724213) has no date.
- **Pass names**: the tours and gag sell "cultpass ELITE" (end card "Get cultpass ELITE"); the Fitstart square and vertical cuts sell "cultpass PLAY" (sports: pools, coaches, partners); c01 sells "Cult" and "Explore cult" with no pass named. The PLAY ad text promises "guaranteed partners" and "temperature-controlled pools"; the video shows only a swimming astronaut.
- **Free-trial claims against the video**: headline "Claim your FREE GYM TRIAL" on 1114356351358840 while the voice says "Buy cultpass ELITE now"; the same headline on 1644460903932522 and 1088984333744777 while the end card says "Best prices + Bonus offers" and nobody mentions a trial. Against that, c01 speaks "two free group workouts" (1529622859191185, 4606351752947622, 2404800830259411, 28835523932756470) while ad text and card show only a paid price; the c02 app screen shows "2/2 FREE GROUP TRIALS LEFT" and nobody says it.
- **Footage against the city advertised**: the c03 shopfront has a Kannada-script sign, yet it runs under "Cult Mall Road, Amritsar!" (1763133728096239), VIP Road (1088984333744777) and Indira Nagar (1644460903932522). The c02 app screen reads "Cult Vaishali Nagar" under ads for Indore (1114356351358840), Amritsar (1142965181654720), VIP Road (1645883207287485) and Prahlad Nagar (1652879219724213). 1182726674293191 says "Cult Neo Gym" while the sign reads "cult.fit / GYM | GROUP CLASSES" and its headline "Top Fitness Spot in Your City!" names no city. 1950951139220699 names "Impackt Fitness Kondapur" and "Hyderabad's Top Fitness Spot!" over a national ELITE sale card.
- **Formats**: "5+ workout formats" (4606351752947622, 1652879219724213); the voice lists five items, the frames show four.
- **Campaign name**: ad text says "Fitstart Sale", the video says "Fitstart Days".
