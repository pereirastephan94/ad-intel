# Cult.fit: concepts

60 ads (all video, no image ads; there is no statics folder) fall into 12 concepts, with one ad not yet written up. Counts are ads in ads.json whose `video_asset` equals the asset id. A concept's count is the sum over its assets, and the counts add up to 60, the number of ads in ads.json (8 + 9 + 6 + 8 + 1 + 7 + 3 + 4 + 1 + 10 + 1 + 1 = 59, plus 1 ad whose video has no write-up). The first five concepts are the earlier pass; c03 gained a second asset, and c06 to c12 are new. Rules: the same footage in another shape or length, or with another price or city on the end card, is one concept. Same footage with new ad text and a new link (1278969961076851 under c03) is also one concept. The Fitstart barbell version (c05) is new footage with a new pass, so it is its own concept, though it shares the c04 layout.
