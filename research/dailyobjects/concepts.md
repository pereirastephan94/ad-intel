# DailyObjects: concepts

30 ads (29 video ads on 29 video assets, 1 dynamic-creative ad with no video asset) fall into 30 concepts. Counts are ads in ads.json whose `video_asset` equals the asset id; no asset runs in more than one ad. The one ad with no video asset (3068383610032307, a DCO ad with 2 cards) is counted from its ad text and landing page and is marked as not written up (c22). A concept's count is the sum over its assets, and the counts add up to 30, the number of ads in ads.json (thirty 1s). Rules: the same footage in another shape is one concept (no pair in this sample qualifies). The same template with a new product is a new concept (c01 and c02 share the Pivot studio pack-up, not the product). A different hook or different footage for the same product is a new concept (c24, c25 and c26 all sell the Array organiser through three different films). No two videos share a script or footage, so no `script_twin` is set. Concentration is nil: every idea runs as a single ad, so the sample is 30 separate ideas rather than a few ideas re-sent.

## Numbers that disagree across ads

- **Loop capacity and wording**: 28858961853760943 (c17) shows three Loop sizes, 5,000, 10,000 and 20,000 mAh, and calls them "true MagSafe". 2219639381939147 (c19) and 2006853093331617 (c20) print only "10000 mAh" on the unit, and c19's ad text says "Qi2-certified" and "MagSafe Compatible". 1757183232006825 (c21) says "20,000" in speech (marked [?]). 1788239835589553 (c18) says 10,000 mAh and "charge my phone twice in one day". "15W" is spoken in c17 and written in c19's text; no frame shows a reading.
- **Which phone**: 4594272790820701 (c09) says and captions "iPhone 17 Pro" over a landing page for the iPhone 18 collection. 1082503661262265 (c08) says "iPhone 18 Pro" aloud while its ad text and link say "iPhone 18". 1737526587462512 (c06) names the iPhone 18 Pro only in the ad text.
- **Product against the link**: 1788239835589553 (c18) spends about half the script on Stack but its ad text and link name only the Loop. 1757183232006825 (c21) features a Loop yet links to the home page. 1436336131960853 (c04) shows no named bag and names bags only in the ad text. 1015591378165168 (c23) says "Power that moves when you do" over a film with no battery, charger or phone.
- **Same code, two products**: "DOXNABEEL5" is in 28858961853760943 (Loop) and 1082503661262265 (Stack cases); neither states what it takes off.
- **Claims with no proof on screen**: Stack "has won international design awards" in 1668887338277124 (no badge, name or year); "seven expressions" and "70 year-old artist" in the 1664136018471636 ad text against two designs and no age on screen; "Our biggest Case collection yet" in 2147362132562237 against six named colours; "Camera Control button" in the 1737526587462512 ad text with no button visible; "anti-yellowing" and "scratch-proof" in 1082503661262265 with no test shown.
- **Ad text missing**: 2006853093331617 (c20) has no ad text, headline or link description, so its Qi2, 15W and MagSafe claims in the sister Loop ad (2219639381939147) are absent there.
- **Carousel clip**: 1145242458447678 (c10) is a 6-card carousel in the Library; the 2.5 s clip is one card and the other five are not in the data. Its headline "peacharchive.zip" is the only headline in the sample.
