## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Works:
- Reads as a stranger's recommendation, not an ad.
- Countable benefit (8 visits) and a named pain (spend minimums).
- Quick cuts hold attention.

Weak:
- Card never shown; the FD requirement arrives late, at 25 s.
- Sells a credit card, so the FD is secondary.
- Tiny disclaimer sits against "100% approval".
