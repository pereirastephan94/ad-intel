## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Works:
- Opens on a relatable problem (cravings, emotional eating), not on a number or offer.
- Explains how the plan worked (stress, a simple meal plan, a mind coach), which the other Fitelo videos skip.
- "Maintained" answers the usual fear of regaining weight.
- Reuses the common end card, so it is cheap to produce.

Weak:
- Final weight is unclear and may not match 30 kg; no timeframe.
- No name, no customer voice; the narrator is anonymous.
- The offer is not spoken, and the deadline on the card has passed while the ads still run.
- First cut at 7.8 s and few captions in the middle.
- Offer limits conflict: date on the card, first 100 in the ad text.
