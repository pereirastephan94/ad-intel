# Fitelo: concepts

34 ads (30 video ads on 21 video assets, 4 image ads) fall into 23 concepts. Counts are ads in ads.json whose `video_asset` equals the asset id; image ads count 1 each. A concept's count is the sum over its assets and images, and the counts add up to 34, the number of ads in ads.json (1 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 7 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1). Rules: the same footage in another shape or length, or with another end card, is one concept (c04, the Body Map pair c16). The same template with a different customer is a new concept, with `script_twin` kept. A customer story told as photos (V4) and the same customer told as a presenter plus testimonial (V1) are different concepts (c09 and c21 are both Amanpreet; c11 and c19 are both Ankita Chaturvedi).

## Series and recurring devices

- **Cashback cheque prop.** A collage of cheques stamped "100 % CASHBACK OFFER" and "WINNER" (amounts about ₹4,250 to ₹16,000, one in the name Ankita Chaturvedi for ₹12000) appears in eight videos and 16 ads: 1449280067157528 (c20), 1912200749756133 and 2996269977403566 (c02), 1637013901394490 and 1130206799365682 (c13), 970142368900928 (c21), 1146824311226546 (c19), 1550858456795923 (c17), the seven c05 ads, and in c03 as a family holding one cheque for "NISHTHA DUBEY" with "CEO, FITELO" printed under the signature (1685798322850756). The spoken offer in all but c03 is 10X, not 100%.
- **Customer story, then the same cashback end card.** Ten videos (19 ads) hand over to the same grey-haired man in a Fitelo polo pointing at "Lose weight, win 10X cashback" (or "win upto ₹5,00,000/-"), "first 100 customers only" and a Book Now button: c02, c05, c11, c12, c13, c14, c17, c19, c20, c21. Nothing in the ads names him. The same man poses in the three people images (c16 twice, c18) and on the c04 Comment YES card.
- **Named-customer weight caption template.** c08, c09 and c11 open on a full-length customer photo with the name, town and a start weight ("VISHAL, Bareilly, UP, WEIGHT 130 kgs", "AMANPREET KAUR, Punjab, 98 kgs", "ANKITA CHATURVEDI, Delhi, 103.6kgs") and no after weight. c08 and c09 end on the identical "GET UPTO 30% OFF ON WEIGHTLOSS PLANS" card on a pale swirl, and c06 reuses that card after a voiceover story. All four have ad text "<Name> achieved his/her fitness goals" except c11.
- **Same ad text on different videos.** One "INDIA'S BIGGEST WEIGHT-LOSS OFFER IS HERE" text with the 10X, first 100 and "Trusted by 2 Lakh+ Indians" headline sits on 18 ads over nine videos (two ads carry a near-identical version); one "<Name> achieved ... Transformation is possible with ... 1000+ experienced coaches" text sits on the six customer-name ads (1685798322850756, 1777858350163803, 1711017009950319, 2190284715234477, 1129807916396583, 970142368900928) and, without a name, on 1461564966113780 and 1577870316987974.
- **Catalogue placeholders.** 1033007776294158, 925451983928233 and 1620379199757741 carry unfilled `{{product.brand}}` text; they were tagged from the creative alone.
- **Small print line.** "In 4 weeks, a typical Fitelo user can expect to lose 2-3 Kgs. ... Individual results may vary." runs over the customer videos and drops off the offer card in c05, c08, c09, c06, c11, c17, c19. It is absent from c15, c23 and from both Body Map images (c16).

## Numbers that disagree across ads

- **Kilos and timeframe against the small print ("2-3 kg in 4 weeks", about 2.5 kg a month for a typical user).** 1449280067157528: 28 kg in 6 months (about 4.7 kg a month). 4832221937013486: 25 kg in 90 days (about 8 kg a month, "4XL to L"). The seven c05 ads (4723519161196734, 995872249468747, 1836729131107152, 1017982897981736, 1116659684664030, 1130303452837399, 2219477718950780): "90 din mein 25-26 kg" (about 8 kg a month) while the opening caption asks "Want to lose 20kg?" and the weights given are 87 to 60 kg, which is 27. 1685798322850756: 25 kgs in around 5 months (about 5 kg a month). 2605110029938293 and 915290991413947: "lose 10 kg in 90 days" with no small print at all, which is about the top of what 2-3 kg every 4 weeks adds up to. Claims with no timeframe: 35, 35 and 25 kg (1912200749756133), 38 kg (1777858350163803), 18 kg (1637013901394490), 30 kg (970142368900928, 2148142946075233), 26 kg (1146824311226546), 21.5 kg (1550858456795923).
- **10X against 100% against up to 5 lakh.** 1912200749756133 says "10 times cashback" and "up to 5 lakh rupees" aloud over cheques reading "100 % CASHBACK OFFER" for about ₹4,250 to ₹16,000, and its end card says only "10X". 4832221937013486 ends on "win upto ₹5,00,000/-" with no 10X while its ad text says 10X. 1685798322850756 shows only the 100% cheque and its ad text has no cashback. 1577870316987974 has the woman say last year's offer was "100% cashback" and "that is old", this year "ten times bigger".
- **First 100 against first 200.** 1577870316987974: the woman says the offer is for the first 200 (heard with doubt) while its ad text twice says "FIRST 100 customers only"; every other cashback ad says first 100. None of these gives a date.
- **The 31 October deadline on ads still live.** 2148142946075233 (24 days running), 29321645884190090 (20 days, started on 31 October itself) and 2166915778033433 (24 days) end on "Offer Valid Till 31st October only"; all three were still listed active on 20 November. The other 10X ads say only "first 100".
- **"This week" and "3 days" on scale ads still live.** 2941541369549817 and 2008324226545509 say the price is for this week and have run 35 days; 925451983928233 says "In next 3 dino" and "first 100 users" and has run 129 days; 926250147231507 says "Only 50 Scales Left".
- **18 against 25 scale metrics.** 1033007776294158 says "overall 18 metrics"; 2008324226545509 says "18 + 7 New Health Metrics" and 25+; 2941541369549817 says 25 aloud over an Amazon page reading "Track 18+ Body Parameters"; 925451983928233 says "ab aap 18 nahi balki 25" and its end card strikes through 18, while its Amazon page still says 18+.
- **Scale old price 5000 against 4999.** 2941541369549817 says and shows ₹5000 struck through; 925451983928233 says 5000 but its end card shows ₹4999; 2008324226545509 and 926250147231507 (ad text) say 4999. All say 999 now.
- **Body Map parameters.** 1449280067157528 shows "Fitelo Body Map / 100+ Parameters Analysis"; 2605110029938293 and 915290991413947 sell the Body Map Review as 50+ parameters.
- **The same photo under two names and stories.** The woman captioned "Malika, Lost 35 kgs" in 1912200749756133 is the woman in 1777858350163803, where the ad text calls her "Mallika" and the video says 104 kg to 66 kg (38 kg). The same footage runs unnamed in 1620379199757741.
- **"Free" against what is sold.** 1685798322850756 and 1146824311226546 open on "Mera weight loss free ho gaya", which only makes sense with cashback; 1685798322850756's ad text and headline never mention it.
- **Headline against video.** 1777858350163803's headline is "Book Now & Get upto 30% OFF" while the video asks viewers to comment YES; 1129807916396583 shows 30% off in the video only, with the headline "Unlock a Healthy Lifestyle Today".
- **Same number, different people.** "108" is the start weight of an unnamed woman in 2148142946075233 and 29321645884190090 and the caption on a heavier man's photo in 1461564966113780.
