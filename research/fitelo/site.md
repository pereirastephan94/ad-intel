## Results and scale claims, as the site states them
- Pace: "An estimate for a typical member following their plan: a quicker first fortnight, then about half a kilo a week." "95% of members start to see results in week two." Slider example: 12 kg in 5 months.
- Member stories on the home page: 24 kg in 3 months, 30 kg in 6 months, 27 kg in 6 months, 17 kg in 279 days, 46 kg in 9 months, 20 kg in 12 months, 18 kg in 12 months; short quotes with 5 to 12 kg. Each block ends "Individual results vary. Figures as reported by members."
- Headline "Tech that guarantees results."; terms: Fitelo "claims no Guarantee of results" and "Results may vary".
- "2 lakh+ users across 70+ countries", "2 lakh+ transformations", "4.9 Google rating", "99.3% satisfaction", "10 years", "800+ coaches and experts" (plans page: "800+ Coaches & Experts"), "As seen on Shark Tank India".
- Terms: "a non-refundable fixed administrative fees which is 40% of the respective plan price"; premium plans are not refundable.
- The site names two co-founders and other staff; no sampled ad names them, so this file does not either.

## How the ads differ from the site
- Cashback: no 10X, 100%, "up to 5 lakh" or "first 100 customers" offer appears on any page read. 21 of 34 sampled ads carry one.
- Coaches: ads say "1000+ experienced coaches"; the site says 800+.
- BodyMap: ads sell a "Body Map Review" at ₹199 with "50+ parameters" (one video shows "100+ Parameters Analysis"); the site says 100+ parameters and lists the test as included in the app.
- Typical result: the ads' small print says "In 4 weeks, a typical Fitelo user can expect to lose 2-3 Kgs"; the site says about half a kilo a week after a quicker first fortnight. The two agree; the ads' headline kilos (25 to 38 kg) are individual stories, as the site's are.
- "10 year anniversary" (ads) matches "10 years in business" (site); "Trusted by 2 lakh+ Indians across 70+ countries" (ad text on 18 ads) matches "2 lakh+ users across 70+ countries" (site).
