## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Why it may work:
- The hook is an outcome with a surprising time frame ("In just their first year") and is repeated as the closing line, so the one idea is hard to miss.
- It moves quickly from claim to named examples. Recognisable employer names and specific roles (AI automations, product, summer analyst, sales and marketing) are more persuasive than a general "great internships" line, for students and for parents.
- The pace suits a young audience: 6.18 cuts per 10 s, a likely music bed with a strong beat, and a rapid logo and face montage on the company list.
- The students in the b-roll are many and varied, and several appear in Mesa-branded clothing or in front of Mesa backdrops. That makes the cohort feel real and gives a school-leaver people to picture themselves beside.
- Constant labelling: "Offline UG Program" sits next to the logo on every frame, and the end card adds "On-campus | Bangalore". A viewer who sees only two seconds still learns what is being sold and that it is an in-person course.
- The podcast set (studio mic, dark wall, speaker looking at a host) borrows the feel of a podcast clip, a format this audience already watches, while the branded polo keeps it clearly Mesa.

Where it is weak:
- No scale. "Our students have landed" could mean five students or the whole class. There is no number, percentage or cohort size anywhere.
- The proof is loose. Students are unnamed, faces are not tied to the internships described, and only one company logo is confirmed in the sampled frames. At about 0.7 s per shot, the company list may go by too fast to read.
- "Full-time internships" in the first year of an undergraduate course will prompt a question from parents (during term or in the summer break?) that the video does not answer. One example is called a "summer analyst", which suggests summer, but it is not stated.
- The speaker is not named or titled, and he never looks at the lens or asks the viewer to do anything. The only call to action is a silent 5-second end card.
- The ads carry no text and no headline, so there is nothing to catch a viewer who scrolls with the video paused or muted before the captions start, and nothing to carry keywords such as the degree name, city or intake.
- The caption error at 2 s ("of the / the undergrad course") is in the most-watched part of the video.
