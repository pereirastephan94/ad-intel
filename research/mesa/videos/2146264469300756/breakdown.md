## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Why it may work:
- The opening line is concrete and unusual for a business school ("sell 5 lakhs worth of product" in "eight weeks"), and it withholds the school's name, so the viewer has a reason to keep watching.
- It looks like a podcast clip, not an advert: studio mic, off-camera eye line, no logo for the first 38 s. That borrows the trust people give to podcast content.
- The endorsement comes from a third party who states investor credentials, and it is framed from the employer's side ("I tell every founder to hire from there"). For an applicant, hearing what employers want and then that Mesa students have it is stronger than the school saying "we place students".
- The argument is tight: founders' problem, the profile they want, a school that produces that profile, the one mechanism that proves it, the hiring number.
- The rewind effect at 7.4 s and the "Hiring." flash at 16 s reset attention at two points where viewers usually drop.
- Captions are large and boxed, so the script is readable with sound off.

Where it is weak:
- The speaker is never named or titled on screen and the ad text does not name him. Anyone who does not recognise him has only his own word for "200 plus startups" and "800 crores".
- None of the numbers (5 lakhs, 150 plus startups, 800 crores) is backed by anything visible in the frames: no logos of hiring startups, no sales evidence.
- The script talks to founders ("when you hire from Mesa"), but the ad targets applicants (MBA entrance exam lookalike audiences in the campaign names). It never says what the student gets: no salary, role, fee or outcome.
- The ad headline ("The only B-school teaching AI agents & no-code") does not match the video's message (sales and hiring). The viewer gets two different pitches.
- No spoken call to action and no deadline; the end card is 6 s of silence on black.
- "Started by my friends" tells the viewer the endorsement is not independent.
- The hook line is repeated in full at 52.7 s, so 7 to 8 s of a 90 s video is duplicate content.
