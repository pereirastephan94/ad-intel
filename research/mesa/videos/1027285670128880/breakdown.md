## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Why it may work
- The first 7.8 s are a known investor making a bold prediction, which earns attention before any selling starts. Mesa borrows his authority for its own thesis.
- "Finally, somebody said it" is a natural reaction line, and the phone selfie with a visible clip mic looks like a creator post, not a brochure.
- The argument is a clean chain: companies shrink, MNC jobs go, two roles remain, both need zero-to-one skills, schools do not teach them, Mesa does.
- Ad text and headline carry the same thesis as the video, so the message lines up from feed to link.
- Mesa keeps relaunching this edit: it now exists in 9:16, square and 4:5, and this ad has 3 copies. That suggests the creative earns its place, though no results are in this data.

Where it is weak
- The biggest numbers ("95% of jobs in MNCs", "30 employees", "almost one crore") have no source, and "one crore" is ambiguous.
- The hook rests on a third party's clip and name. Nothing in the frames shows permission, and the speaker has no connection to Mesa stated.
- 105 s at 187 words per minute with no stat cards is a lot to follow. A viewer who drops at 30 s has heard the problem but not what Mesa does.
- In this 4:5 version the picture is a narrow card on a grey canvas, so faces and captions are small on a phone.
- The Mesa speaker has no name or title on screen.
- The link goes to the homepage, not a programme or application page.
