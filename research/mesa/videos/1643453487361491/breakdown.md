## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Why it may work
- It opens on the number one objection to any new business school (placements) and answers it in the next breath with "100% placements".
- It explains how, not just what: a full-time professional careers team, 500+ startup relationships, curriculum changed to match hiring needs, one-on-one prep. A mechanism makes a big claim easier to believe.
- The contrast with student-run placement committees is specific and insider knowledge that MBA applicants will recognise.
- A staff member who owns the outcome ("my team") is speaking, which gives accountability.
- The IIMB logo and green number give the eye something to hold while the claims are made.

Where it is weak
- "100% placements" and "fantastic salaries" have no figure, batch, year, company name or source on screen. This is the weakest point for a sceptical viewer and a possible compliance risk.
- The speaker has no name or title on screen.
- Ad text and headline are about classmates and co-founders, while the video is about placements. The button says "See details" while the video says "Apply Now". The messages do not line up.
- It attacks "other business schools" without evidence, which can read as defensive.
- No student or employer voice. All proof is the school talking about itself.
- Only 1 ad and 1 copy, in an awareness campaign, so there is no sign in this data that it has been scaled.
