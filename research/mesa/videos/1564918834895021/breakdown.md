## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Why it may work:
- The hook is strong and specific: it promises a number and positions rivals as hiding it, in 3.5 seconds, with the speaker's face close to the lens and no cut to break attention.
- It pays the hook off at once with a precise figure (57.5%, not "over half") shown as a green card. Precise decimals read as real data.
- It turns a possible weakness into the selling point. Lower pay than a rival offer is reframed as a choice ("learning over a paycheck", ESOPs, "first millionaires"), which pre-empts the salary objection for an audience that compares CTC.
- Proof-style footage is dense: 37 cuts, students, classrooms, guest speakers and Mesa wall signs, so the claims about campus and founders are at least illustrated.
- Branding is on every frame three ways: corner logo, chest logo on the polo, wall signs. Direct eye contact and "our students" make it feel like the school's own leadership speaking.

Where it is weak:
- The numbers have no source. No cohort year, base size or placement report is shown, and "many" turned down higher offers is not quantified. A sceptical viewer cannot check any of it.
- "Joined super early stage startups" under 30 people is presented as the hardest job to land, but many viewers and parents will read small startups and offers below alternatives as risk. No salary figure is given even though Mesa names the creative "salary".
- The speaker has no name or title on screen, and none of the founders or guest speakers in the b-roll is identified, so the "100 plus founders" claim stays anonymous.
- Captions are small, thin and placed mid-frame, and several frames have no caption at all. With sound off, the 30 people, 100 founders, 1.5x and 54% numbers may be missed, since only 57.5% is seen as a card in the frames.
- No spoken call to action and no urgency. The end card is about 5.5 s of the 58 s.
- The ad wrapper does not match the video: the headline is about "AI agents & no-code" and the text about Bengaluru and 100+ startups, with nothing about placements or ESOPs, and the click goes to the generic homepage. It is running as one option in a multi-creative (DCO) ad with a single copy, started 2026-11-18, so there is no sign yet of it being scaled.
