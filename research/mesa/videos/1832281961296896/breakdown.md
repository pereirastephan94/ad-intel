## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Why it may work:
- The opening is a number and a strong claim in the first second, with the number highlighted in gold, and no intro or greeting to skip past.
- The problem is framed so the viewer is not at fault ("genuine cases where these folks cannot quit their jobs"), which fits salaried 25 to 38 year olds, the targeting named in the campaign.
- Headline, ad text and script all repeat one idea: build a business without quitting your job. The message is consistent from feed to video to landing page.
- Proof comes right after the mechanism and uses three concrete items (ARR, capital raised, TV show appearances).
- B-roll of real students pitching and selling makes "build their own businesses" visible rather than only stated.
- Square format with large captions works with sound off; with little or no music, the captions carry it.

Where it is weak:
- The speaker is never named or given a title on screen or in the ad text, so the authority behind "we" is unclear to a cold viewer.
- All proof numbers are spoken or captioned only; no company names, screenshots or sources. The numbers come from Mesa students in general, not from the part-time programme.
- No spoken call to action, and the last 6.5 s is a silent static card, which is about 11% of the runtime with nothing happening.
- The end card says "PGP in Business, AI & Entrepreneurship" without "Ascend", "Hybrid", "weekends" or duration, so it does not distinguish the part-time programme from the full-time one.
- Key qualifiers (12 months, weekends, 5+ years of experience) are only in the ad text, not in the video.
- Zero cuts in the first 5 s and an eyeline away from the lens make the opening depend entirely on the line and the caption.
