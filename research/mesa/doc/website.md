Mesa sells three programmes from one site, mesaschool.co. Each has its own promise, and the ads send people to the matching page.

### The full-time PGP (the main product)

- **Name:** PGP in Startup Leadership & Entrepreneurship. 12 months, full-time, residential, Bangalore. Next intake August 2027, 175 to 200 seats.
- **Main line:** "The business school where you build real businesses."
- **Outcome numbers on the page:** ₹26L average CTC (the page says audited by B2K Analytics for cohorts 1 and 2), 2.8X salary jump, 58% in founder-facing roles, ₹36.5L for the top 25%, ₹45L highest, 100% placement, 500+ partner startups.
- **Six pillars:** new-age academics; learn from 100+ founders; AI and product (25+ tools, 3 AI products built); a ₹5 Cr in-house fund; ₹1L in sales in the first 4 weeks; 250+ hours of career prep.
- **Fees:** ₹25.35L early bird, rising to ₹27.85L, plus GST. Accommodation ₹2.5L to ₹4.5L extra.
- **Admission:** its own test, the MAT (Mesa Admissions Test), then an interview. Interview fee ₹1,500. Six named scholarships.
- **Borrowed credibility:** backers named on the page include Kunal Shah, Vidit Aatrey and Vijay Shekhar Sharma. Series A of ₹110 Cr from Elevation and Dharana.
- **Live event:** an info session titled "Building India's Stanford" with Varun Limaye on 18 Nov.

### Ascend (for people with jobs)

- **Name:** Hybrid PGP in Business, AI & Entrepreneurship. 12 months, weekends, part-time, about 10 hours a week.
- **Main line:** "Don't quit your job."
- **Four people it speaks to:** someone who wants a leadership role, someone who wants to switch careers, someone who wants to start a business, someone in a family business.
- **Proof points:** ₹5L revenue in 8 weeks in "Build Your Own Business", 3 live projects, lifelong membership.

### Undergraduate

- **Name:** 4-year UG in Business Management & Entrepreneurship.
- **Proof points:** a 6-week Silicon Valley immersion and 3 founder's office internships.

### How the site and the ads fit together

- The site leads with numbers. The ads mostly do not. The most-used PGP video gives no salary or placement figure at all; the proof waits on the landing page.
- The words are the same everywhere: "build", "builders", "founders", "startup". "MBA" appears mainly as the thing Mesa is not.
- PGP ads go to the home page or a short landing page. Ascend ads go to the Ascend page. Every ad button says "Apply now" (156 of 163).
