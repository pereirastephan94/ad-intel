**The short version:** Mesa added most of its live ads in the last three days. It runs three programmes side by side, reuses a small set of videos across many audiences, and sends everything to an "Apply now" button.

### A sudden ramp

- 114 of the 163 ads collected started on 16, 17 or 18 November. Only 2 are older than 9 November.
- Another team's count had Mesa at 16 live ads on 12 November and 265 on 18 November. This pull agrees with that shape.
- So most of what is live is days old. Nothing here has run long enough to be called a proven winner. The safer signal is what Mesa chose to copy the most.

### Three programmes, three sets of ads

- **Ascend (part-time, for working people):** the largest share, about half the ads. Headlines: "Start your own business while you keep your job", "No need to quit your job", "Stuck in a rut at work? Start here".
- **PGP (full-time):** headlines "Your classmates could be your co-founders", "The only B-school teaching AI agents & no-code", "The B-school built for builders".
- **Undergraduate:** a small set, video only, often with no ad text at all.

### Who they aim at (read from Mesa's own campaign names)

Mesa leaves its internal campaign names in the links. They show the audiences:

- **People who look like past leads:** lookalikes built from an XAT list, an MH-CET MBA list, its own admission-test takers, "8K SQLs" (8,000 sales-qualified leads), and a list of leads with work experience.
- **Interests:** tech and AI, sales and key-account managers, "career aspirants", "corporate professionals", and odd ones such as shopping and trading.
- **Retargeting:** a campaign tagged `wv-sme`, most likely people who visited the website.
- **Let Facebook decide:** Advantage+ campaigns with no targeting, including one limited to ages 24 to 30.
- **Awareness:** one campaign aimed broadly, excluding existing leads.
- **Ascend:** aimed at ages 25 to 38, split into business, AI and open audiences.

### How they name and test creatives

Each creative has a name such as `video_general_vsl_200-cohort` or `video_career-outcomes_vsl_bschools-placements_v2`. The pattern is format, theme, style, topic, version. Read as a list, it is Mesa's own map of its angles:

- **Themes:** general, career outcomes, admissions, pedagogy.
- **Styles:** "vsl" (one person selling to camera), influencer, student story, "aa" (animated explainer comparing CAT and Mesa's test).
- **Versions:** the same video is re-cut and re-run as v1, v2, v3. The placements video exists in three cuts, with the opening trimmed by 1.6 seconds in one of them.

### Formats

- About a third of ads are plain video, a third plain image, and a third "mixed" ads where Facebook picks from several images, videos and texts.
- Many vertical videos are the square version with blurred bands added above and below, not a reshoot.
- The same video runs under several different headlines, so the ad text and the video often do not match each other.

### What is still unknown

- Spend, reach and results are not public for these ads. Nothing here says which ad performs best.
- This pull has 163 of roughly 320 live ads. The counts will shift as the rest come in.
