Top FAQs
Who is eligible to apply to Mesa UG program?
All students completing their class 12th exams in 2027 can apply to the program. It is also open to students who completed class 12th in 2026.
We are specifically looking for students, who believe they are meant for bigger things, who learn by doing things versus just sitting in class, who want to be surrounded by a top peer group and want to be challenged. Our student alumni include folks who went to Shark Tank, Harvard MUN winners, national level athletes & dancers, ex-founders, academic toppers, etc.
How is the admission process for Mesa UG different from other programs?
Our admission process is designed to select the best students and to assess students holistically across past achievements, hunger & potential, aptitude, communication, ability to work with others & desire to dream big. Hence, the process also includes several stages - MSAT (Mesa Scholarship & Admissions Test), an AI interview, and offline interviews on campus, comprising a Team Case Challenge and a Senior Leadership Round for case/business pitch. We are looking for top 1% students in the country, who will go on to do amazing things in life - if you believe you are one of them, apply now!
Is Mesa UGC-accredited, and will I get a degree?
Mesa School is not accredited with UGC and does not provide a degree or diploma to students. This allows our curriculum to be updated frequently to match the industry, with big components of experiential & learn by doing with practitioner led learning; versus getting fixed in a decade old curriculum design.
Most of our students choose to enroll in online degree programs to pursue a degree in parallel to the Mesa School program. Most common universities in which our past students have enrolled in include IIM Bangalore, IIM Udaipur and Narsee Monjee.
According to the Indian government ruling, online degrees from top 100 ranked universities are considered equivalent to offline degree programs from all universities. These online degrees from top 100 universities can be used to apply for Masters in India and abroad, government jobs and all jobs.
Is accommodation mandatory, and what does it include?
No, student accommodation is optional but it is a residential program so students will require to move to Bangalore and come to campus daily. For those who opt in, our facilities are located within 2 km of campus, and include 24/7 security with CCTV, an on-site infirmary and nurses, shuttle service, laundry, gym access, and recreational/study spaces. There are several room size options for students to choose from, details of which can be provided based on your interest in opting for student living.
Does Mesa offer scholarships and financial aid?
Yes. Scholarships of up to 75% on tuition are available, and the amount you unlock depends on your MSAT and interview performance. There are six types: Means-based, Merit-based, Trailblaz-her, Sports, Maverick and Bob the Builder. Loan-partner financing is also available, covering up to 100% of tuition and accommodation over tenures up to 12 years.
What's the ROI, is the fee worth it?
Mesa UG program is designed to create top 1% young professionals in the country - and that happens with a very high quality program, delivered by top faculty & industry practitioners & access to personalized mentorship. In four years, students build 4 real businesses to create 10 lac in revenue (a D2C brand, a Gen-AI product, a YouTube channel, and their own startup), complete 3 Founder's Office internships, 400+ hours of AI curriculum & hands-on product building, get direct access to founders, operators and investors as mentors. Students do full time industry immersion in their last year. All this puts Mesa students way ahead of their peer group at that age and sets them up for success in the current world of AI.
We offer scholarships to deserving candidates based on their application/interview strength and financial needs. We encourage you to submit your application, for us to review your scholarship case.
What kind of outcomes can I expect after Mesa UG program?
We expect Mesa students to be able to choose from a variety of outcomes - either they will become full time entrepreneurs or join a fast moving organization in a high impact role for a few years or pursue their masters or other passion areas. The program will expose them to building real revenue businesses and also working in internships in top companies - so at the end, they will have both the capability & options to choose what excites them the most. 2000+ startups, founders, operators are part of Mesa School network in Bangalore, which opens up to all students. Our last 3 PG cohorts have averaged 26 lacs in annual CTC and our first year UG students did internships with average stipends of 30-50k INR while building businesses in their first year which cumulatively crossed 2 Cr INR in revenue.
Should I apply to the program if I am not from a science/tech background and unsure if I will be able to learn AI?
Absolutely, you should apply. First, learning AI is no longer optional - in today's world, increasingly, you are expected to be great at using AI in all jobs, sectors & role functions. Second, AI is taught in a unique way at Mesa School. Irrespective of your past exposure, the program is designed to be hands-on and allows you to learn at your pace. 400+ hours of AI curriculum & hands-on building, in hackathon style while supported by mentors throughout and a peer group that is in the same boat as you, learning & helping each other - is what allows our students to become great at AI in no time. Our students have built AI products doing crores in revenue and also applied AI in real businesses to solve actual problems. And none of them knew AI before they started at Mesa School.
Will I be able to choose from multiple global immersions?
Students are provided several options for global immersion at both our partner university campuses (UC Berkeley USA, LSE London, NUS Singapore) and industry visits (Singapore, Dubai, South Korea, China). These are optional and students can opt in based on their availability across all years of the program.
