Epigamia
As competition heated up in the high-protein yogurt space, Epigamia needed to decide whether to double down or pivot its strategy. Mesa students led market research, 100+ consumer interviews, and taste tests to uncover insights, then built a 24-month roadmap across brand, product, and growth.
Two students from this team later joined Epigamia as Chiefs of Staff.
Blue Tokai
Blue Tokai wanted to scale its café footprint smarter, not faster, by choosing locations backed by data. Mesa students built a site selection framework that blended demographics, traffic, competition, and economics into one decision-making model.
Their prototype tool helped identify high-potential clusters for expansion.
Qila Games
Qila Games, an India-based studio, aimed to enter the Middle East by positioning its multiplayer game as both a gaming and social platform.
Mesa students built a complete GTM strategy covering branding, product design, user acquisition and retention, and competitive differentiation in the MENA region.

Financing
We offer various financing options for our students, each designed with flexibility and convenience in mind, allowing you to focus on your studies without financial worry.
Students, once offered a seat, gain access to Mesa's loan partners for short & long term loan options, covering 100% of their tuition & accommodation fees, with up to 12 years in loan tenure.
Frequently Asked Questions
What are the eligibility criteria for the PGP in Startup Leadership & Entrepreneurship?
A bachelor's degree in any discipline and 1+ year of work experience in any domain.
How long is the PGP in Startup Leadership & Entrepreneurship and is it residential?
It is a 12 months, Full-Time, Residential program based in Bangalore, starting Aug 2027.
What is the course fee for the PGP in Startup Leadership & Entrepreneurship?
The course fee ranges from ₹25,35,000 (Early Bird) to ₹27,85,000, depending on your admission round. Fee amounts mentioned are in reference to tuition only, and are exclusive of applicable 18% GST and accommodation expenses.
Are scholarships available?
Mesa considers merit-based scholarships for exceptional candidates in the PGP cohort, as part of its effort to reduce financial barriers for outstanding applicants. Students can also access financing through Mesa's loan partners, covering up to 100% of tuition and accommodation fees, with loan tenures of up to 12 years — subject to partner approval, not guaranteed by Mesa School of Business.
What is the average CTC after Mesa's PGP in Startup Leadership & Entrepreneurship?
Graduates of Mesa's PGP in Startup Leadership & Entrepreneurship report an average CTC of ₹26 lakh, audited by B2K Analytics across Cohorts 1 and 2 — an average 2.8X jump over pre-Mesa compensation. The top 25% of graduates average ₹36.5 lakh, and 58% are placed in founder-facing roles.
