## Offer and call to action
Free live info session on Zoom. Sunday 22 Nov, 12 PM IST. On-image button: Register Now. The ad's own button says Apply now, which does not match. Landing page is the Ascend info session page.

## Why it may work
- Low-commitment ask. Registering for a webinar is easier than applying.
- A real date and time gives a reason to act this week.
- Borrowed credibility from two well-known logos next to a named speaker.
- Body opens with a sharp insight ('around year five, the career question changes') and never hard-sells.

## Where it is weak
- The image headline is generic. 'Thinking About Your Next Move?' could be any course or job ad.
- Ad button says Apply now while the image says Register Now. That can confuse or scare off people who only want the session.
- Link description has a grammar slip: 'For working professional with 5+ yrs of experience'.
- Image says 'hybrid PGP' while other Ascend ads from the same days say 'Online PGP'.
