## Why it may work

- It picks a clear enemy (the MBA) and positions against it in three words.
- The weight contrast, thin then bold, makes the eye land on 'learn business and build one'.
- Two photos show both halves of the promise: learning in a group and pitching on stage.

## Where it is weak

- 'Not another MBA' is a common line in this category and may not feel new.
- The strongest specifics (₹1L capital, ₹5L target, 8 weeks) are only in the body text.
- The photos show in-person, full-time looking settings for a weekend hybrid programme.
