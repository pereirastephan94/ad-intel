## Hook and message
Single idea: you can learn to build a business without leaving your job. For salaried professionals who want to start something but will not give up income. Promise: an online, 12 month PGP that runs alongside your career.

- No numbers on outcomes, no testimonials, no faculty names.

## Offer and call to action
No price, deadline or scholarship on this one. Button on image: Apply Now. Ad button: Apply now. Goes to the Ascend page.

## Why it may work
- The headline removes the biggest objection (quitting) in the first line.
- 'Learn to Build' keeps Mesa's builder positioning while selling a part-time course.
- Clean layout with one photo and one programme pill. Easy to read at feed speed.

## Where it is weak
- No proof at all on the image. No number, name or outcome.
- The photo is a generic office chat and shows an in-person campus, while the product is labelled Online.
- 'Learn to Build' is vague. It does not say what you build or what you leave with.
