## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- It names the reader's main objection and offers a low-effort next step (a free Zoom) instead of an application.
- A named co-founder and a fixed date make it feel real and personal.
- The body copy is the most detailed in the batch about how the programme runs.

Where it is weak:

- The image says 'Register Now!' but the ad button says 'Apply now'. The time is missing from the image.
- The photo shows an in-person group of men only, which does not show 'online' and is not a diverse picture.
- Link description has a grammar slip ('working professional').
