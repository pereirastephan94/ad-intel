## Hook and message
One idea: you can learn to build a business without resigning. It is for employed professionals who see a full-time course as too risky. The promise is a business built alongside the current career.

- Image: 'Online' and '12 Months' are format facts. 'Build Your Business Alongside Your Career' is a promise with no proof shown.
- Body: 'Live classes on weekends, about 10 hours a week, for 12 months'. Format facts.
- Body: 'You learn strategy, finance and sales, and put them to work in a real business and at your own job'. No source or example.

## Offer and call to action
Apply to the Online PGP in Business, AI & Entrepreneurship (Ascend). No price, scholarship or deadline in this ad. Button on the image: Apply Now. Ad button: Apply now.

## Why it may work
- It removes the biggest objection (quitting) in the headline.
- Gold on 'to Learn to Build' puts the emphasis on the benefit, not the fear.
- The body gives a precise time cost, about 10 hours a week.

## Where it is weak
- No proof, number or outcome on the image.
- The wide crop makes the two men small and the photo less striking than in the split layouts.
- 'About 10 hours a week' is only in the body text.
