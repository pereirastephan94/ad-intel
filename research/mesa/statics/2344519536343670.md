## Why it may work
- One big number is the fastest read in the whole batch.

## Where it is weak
- 'Founder-facing roles' is undefined and unsourced. A sceptical reader can dismiss it.
- 58 percent also means 42 percent do not. It is not an obviously high number without context.
- Image talks about job outcomes, while the headline and body talk about classmates and co-founders. Two messages in one ad.
- Soft 'Explore' on the image against a hard 'Apply now' on the button.
