## Hook and message
Single idea: this is the opposite of a classroom MBA. For professionals who are tired of theory and want to run something real. Promise: learn business and build an actual business with a team. The body makes it concrete: ₹1L capital, two teammates and a ₹5L sales target in the first 8 weeks.

## Claims and proof
- Body: 'In your first 8 weeks, you get ₹1L in capital, two teammates and a ₹5L sales target, with real customers'. The most specific mechanism in the batch. Not shown on the image.
- Body: 'learn business from top faculty'. No names.
- '12 months' and 'Weekends' on the image. Programme facts.
- The two photos act as proof of team work and of pitching on a Mesa stage. No numbers on the image.

## Offer and call to action
No price or deadline. Button on image and ad: Apply now. Link description: 'Learn business. Build one.'

## Why it may work
- Clear enemy. 'Not another MBA' sorts the audience in three words.
- The thin and bold type contrast makes the point visually.
- Body copy has the best hook in the set (₹1L capital, ₹5L target, 8 weeks), with real stakes.
- Two photos show both halves of the promise: learning in a group and building on stage.

## Where it is weak
- The ₹1L and ₹5L details are hidden in the body. The image alone is a slogan.
- It calls itself a PGP while saying not an MBA. To many Indian readers a PGP is an MBA equivalent, so the contrast is thin.
- Both photos show an in-person campus and stage, from programmes that already ran, while Ascend is a new weekend programme.
- Dense layout. Two tilted photos plus pill plus chips compete for attention.
