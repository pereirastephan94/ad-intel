## Why it may work

- Specific numbers (8 weeks, ₹1L, ₹5L+, teams of 3) make the exercise feel real and structured.
- Polaroids of students selling real products are warm and believable.
- The light template stands out from the dark creatives in the same account.

## Where it is weak

- The people in the photos wear Mesa polos at a physical stall and look like full-time campus students. The ad sells a weekend programme for working professionals. The proof may be borrowed from another programme.
- The image says 'Hybrid' while other Ascend ads say 'Online'. The naming is inconsistent.
- The ad text sells career exploration ('Try your next move') and an early bird date. The image sells the 8 week business. The two do not line up, and neither the deadline nor the scholarship is on the image.
