## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- The warm, candid photo makes an online course feel social.
- 'Founding cohort' makes newness sound exclusive.
- Same template as the other light Ascend creatives, so the set looks like one family.

Where it is weak:

- No reason given for why founding members are better off.
- The deadline is only in the small link description.
- The photo shows people in a room, in Mesa shirts, which says 'campus' more than 'online'.
