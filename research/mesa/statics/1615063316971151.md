## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- One number, one promise. It is the most direct return-on-fee message in the batch.
- The cap-toss photo shows scale and joy, and shows that a real cohort has graduated.
- The heavy serif number gives it a premium, editorial look.

Where it is weak:

- The claim has no source or base. A multiple from a low base (for example freshers) can mislead.
- No mention of fee, so the reader cannot judge the return.
- Image button says 'Explore the PGP program' but the ad button is 'Apply now'.
