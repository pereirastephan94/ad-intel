## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- A precise, non-round number feels measured, not invented.
- 'Founder-facing' is a fresh, specific outcome that sets Mesa apart from salary-led ads.
- The cheering crowd adds energy and shows a real cohort.

Where it is weak:

- 'Founder-facing roles' is undefined and unsourced.
- The ad text and headline ('Your classmates could be your co-founders') talk about peers, not the 58% claim, so image and copy pull in different directions.
- Image button says 'Explore the PGP program' while the ad button is 'Apply now'.
