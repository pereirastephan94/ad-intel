## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- It removes the biggest barrier (quitting) in the first line.
- The photo looks like two working adults in a real conversation.
- The side panel puts format, length and button in one place.

Where it is weak:

- The headline is long and the sub-line repeats it.
- No proof, outcome or number.
- The idea overlaps with the 'What if' and 'A PGP on weekends' creatives, so they may compete with each other.
