## Why it may work
- It removes the biggest objection (quitting) in the headline.
- The body gives a precise time cost, about 10 hours a week, which makes the commitment easy to judge.
- The light editorial layout looks calm and credible for an older, employed audience.

## Where it is weak
- No proof, number or outcome on the image.
- The photo shows two colleagues talking. It does not show a business being built.
- 'About 10 hours a week', the most useful fact, is only in the body text.
