## Why it may work
- Travel photos break the pattern of classroom shots and feel personal, like a friend's trip post.
- Known logos in the photos (Adobe, Entrepreneurs First) give instant credibility without a written claim.
- Honest about cost and optional status in the body, which avoids a bait feeling.

## Where it is weak
- The people in the photos look like young students, while the ad set targets ages 25 to 38. Ascend is a founding cohort, so these trips come from another Mesa programme.
- Leading with an optional paid extra may attract clicks from people who will not value the main course.
- No call to action and no programme facts on the image. Weekends, 12 months and keep-your-job are all absent.
- The image does not name the cities, which are the main draw.
