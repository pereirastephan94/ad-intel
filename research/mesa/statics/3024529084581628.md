## Why it may work
- Access to investors is a benefit a self-taught founder cannot easily get, and '25+ VCs' puts a number on it.
- The stage photo is real event evidence with a real student product on the screen.
- The three chips at the bottom add mechanism and scale without crowding the headline.

## Where it is weak
- No funding outcome. Pitching to VCs is not the same as raising money, and no result is shown.
- The photo is low resolution and the slide text is too small to read in a feed.
- The image says 'Hybrid PGP' while other Ascend ads from the same days say 'Online PGP', which may confuse someone who sees both.
