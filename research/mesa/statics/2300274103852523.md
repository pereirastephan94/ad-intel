## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- It answers the practical question a mid-career buyer has: what help do I get with my next job.
- The one-to-one photo matches the 1:1 coaching promise directly.
- Three short pills and a button make the benefits readable at feed speed.

Where it is weak:

- No outcome proof. No placements, salaries, partner names or network size.
- It is a founding cohort, so 'for life' and 'alumni' cannot yet be shown for Ascend itself.
- 'Hybrid PGP' here while other Ascend ads say 'Online PGP'. The naming is not consistent.
- Image says 'Lifelong membership', link description says 'Lifelong network'. Small mismatch.
