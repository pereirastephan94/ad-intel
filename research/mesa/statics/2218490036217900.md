## Why it may work
- Three-word headline, huge, readable in a second.
- The time cost is stated up front (about 10 hours a week), which answers the first practical question.
- Body is honest in tone: 'what it asks of you'. That builds trust with experienced people.
- Fixed date creates urgency.

## Where it is weak
- The photo shows only men. That narrows who sees themselves in it.
- 'Don't Quit Your Job.' alone does not say what to do instead. The payoff sits in small text.
- Ad button (Apply now) does not match the image button (Register Now).
- Same link description grammar slip as the sister ad: 'For working professional'.
