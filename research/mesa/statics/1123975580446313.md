## Why it may work

- A rupee figure in the headline is concrete and easy to picture.
- The pitch photo with a real pricing slide shows the work, not just a classroom.
- Short time frame (8 weeks) lowers the perceived effort.

## Where it is weak

- 'Can make' is soft. There is no evidence that any team actually reached ₹5 lakh.
- The speaker is in a suit at an in-person pitch. That does not look like an online weekend programme.
- Deadline, no application fee and scholarship (all in the body) are missing from the image.
