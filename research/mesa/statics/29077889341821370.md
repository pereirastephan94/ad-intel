## Hook and message
Single idea: be first. For people who like being early and want founding-member status. Promise: a place in the first ever Online PGP cohort, starting in 2027.

## Claims and proof
- 'Founding Online PGP Cohort'. This confirms the online programme has not run before.
- 'Cohort Starting in 2027'. No month given.
- Body: 'Early bird applications close Nov 26, 2026'. Deadline.
- Body: 'Scholarships up to ₹10L'. No conditions shown.

## Offer and call to action
Early bird deadline Nov 26, 2026 and scholarships up to ₹10L, both only in the body and link description, not on the image. Button on image: Apply Now. Ad button: Apply now.

## Why it may work
- Text-only cards stand out among photo ads and read instantly.
- 'Founding' turns the weakness of a new programme (no track record) into a status reason to join.
- Link description carries the deadline, so urgency shows right under the image.

## Where it is weak
- The image gives no reason to want the course. It assumes the reader already knows Mesa and Ascend.
- The deadline and scholarship are not on the image. The sister early bird creative fixes this.
- 'Online PGP' with no subject. PGP in what is left unsaid.
