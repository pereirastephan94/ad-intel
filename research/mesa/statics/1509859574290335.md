Where it is weak:

- No outcome, fee or proof is shown. It only says what you avoid.
- 'Hybrid' is not explained on the image, and other ads in this batch call the same programme 'Online'.
- The photo shows an in-person room, which may clash with a weekend, mostly online promise.
