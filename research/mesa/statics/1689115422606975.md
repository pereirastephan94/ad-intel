## Hook and message

At Mesa the course is the company you build. It is for people aged about 24 to 30 who want to start something and find normal MBAs too theoretical. The promise is that you learn business by running a real one.

## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- One short line states the whole positioning and is easy to repeat.
- Three happy students behind real packaged products show the idea without extra words.
- Very little text, with a strong size contrast, so it reads in a second.

Where it is weak:

- 'The only B-school' is an unsupported claim that a sceptical reader can dismiss.
- The image never names the programme, its length, city or who it is for. Nothing says what JaldiBites is or who these people are.
- No button on the image and no outcome proof (revenue, funding, jobs).
