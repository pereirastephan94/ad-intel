## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- Very fast to read. Five words carry the whole idea.
- 'Founding' turns the lack of a track record into a badge of status.
- Deadline and scholarship in the text add urgency.

Where it is weak:

- The image says nothing about what the programme teaches, costs or delivers.
- The deadline and the ₹10L scholarship are missing from the image, where most people look.
- 'Founding cohort' also means no alumni proof.
