## Hook and message
Single idea: a hard revenue number on a short clock. For professionals who want to prove they can sell something, not only study. Promise: build and sell your first product and reach 5 lakh in sales within the first 8 weeks.

## Claims and proof
- 'Generate 5L in just 8 Weeks'. Worded as an outcome. Other Ascend ads in this batch describe 5L as a sales target, not a result. No source, no past cohort result shown.
- Body: 'Your first business could start in your first 8 weeks'. Softer wording than the image.
- Body: 'Early bird applications close Nov 26, 2026'. Deadline.
- Body: 'no application fee and scholarships of up to ₹10L'. Offer terms, no conditions shown.
- The classroom photo of a student pitching a product with pricing is used as visual proof.

## Offer and call to action
Strongest offer in the batch, but only in the body: early bird closes Nov 26, 2026, no application fee, scholarships up to ₹10L. Button on image: Apply Now. Ad button: Apply now.

## Why it may work
- A number plus a deadline (5L, 8 weeks) is the most concrete promise in the Ascend set.
- The photo shows a real student product pitch with a real brand and price, which makes the claim feel doable.
- Body stacks three reasons to act now: deadline, no fee, scholarship.

## Where it is weak
- '5L' with no rupee sign and no word 'revenue' or 'sales' can be misread as salary or profit.
- Stated as a result when it is a target. This invites doubt, or complaints if challenged.
- The deadline and scholarship are missing from the image, where they would do the most work.
- Headline field ('Start your own business while you keep your job') is reused from another ad and does not match the image.
