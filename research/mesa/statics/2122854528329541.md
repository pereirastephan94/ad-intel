## Why it may work
- The strike-through device tells the whole story without reading a sentence. Strong pattern in feed.
- Names the real costs of a full-time course in the reader's own words.
- Hours per week stated. It makes the commitment feel small and honest.
- The photo is warm and has a woman in the lead, unlike most others in the set.

## Where it is weak
- It is all about convenience. Nothing says why this PGP is good, or what you get at the end.
- 'A PGP on weekends' is the category, not Mesa. Any executive programme could run this line.
- The struck-through grey text is low contrast on black and may be skipped.
