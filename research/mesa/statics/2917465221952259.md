## Why it may work
- A question headline invites the reader to answer yes in their head.
- The photo has a real moment in it. The woman's thinking pose matches a 'what if' question.
- Body copy names the audience clearly (5+ years).

## Where it is weak
- No proof on the image. It is a promise with no number or name behind it.
- 'Startup' on the image, 'business' in the headline field. The two words attract different people.
- Image says nothing about weekends or hours. The reader must open the body to learn how it fits a job.
