## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- A specific date gives a clear reason to act now.
- The underlined date is the second thing the eye lands on after the headline.
- The body copy fills in the practical facts.

Where it is weak:

- It never says what you gain by applying early (fee cut, scholarship, priority).
- No photo of people and no programme content on the image.
- Another ad in this batch gives the early bird date as 29 Nov, which weakens trust in the deadline.
