## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- 'AI-native operator' gives the reader an identity to want, not just a course.
- The skills list is concrete and current.
- Using your own job as the project makes the payoff feel immediate and employer-friendly.

Where it is weak:

- No example of a finished project or a result.
- No button, duration or schedule on the image.
- 'Operator' is startup jargon that some corporate readers may not know.
