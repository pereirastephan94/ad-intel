## Why it may work
- The question names the exact fear (quitting) and removes it in one line.
- Bold left-aligned type on black reads fast and the gold panel puts the button in the middle of the image, not at the bottom.
- The tighter crop makes the woman's thoughtful face the focal point, which fits a 'what if' question.

## Where it is weak
- No proof. No outcome, number, founder or student business is shown.
- Three colours of panel (gold, black, green) plus rules make the layout busy.
- Who it is for (5+ years of experience) appears only in the body text.
