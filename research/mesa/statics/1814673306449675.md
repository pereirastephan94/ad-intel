## Hook and message
Single idea: this course ends with you pitching a real startup to investors. For working professionals who want to found something, not only study. Promise: build from idea to first customers with a 1:1 founder mentor, then pitch to 25+ VCs on Demo Day.

## Claims and proof
- '25+ VCs' on Demo Day. No source or list of names shown.
- '~50 startups incubated'. No source, no time frame.
- '1:1 founder mentor'. Stated, no detail.
- Body adds 'validation to MVP, launch and first customers, stage by stage'. Process claim, no proof.
- The photo of a Mesa-branded stage pitch acts as visual proof that a Demo Day has happened.

## Offer and call to action
No price, date or deadline. Offer is access: Startup Lab plus Demo Day inside Ascend. Button on image and on the ad: Apply now.

## Why it may work
- A concrete end point (pitch to 25+ VCs) is easier to picture than 'learn entrepreneurship'.
- The stage photo is real event footage, not stock, and carries the Mesa logo twice.
- Three short proof chips give a skimmer the facts without reading the body.

## Where it is weak
- Ascend is sold elsewhere as a founding cohort starting 2027, so the Demo Day photo and '~50 startups incubated' must come from another Mesa programme. The ad does not say so.
- Nothing on the image says weekends or keep your job, which is the main Ascend benefit. It only appears in the body.
- The startup slide in the photo is busy and pulls the eye away from the headline.
