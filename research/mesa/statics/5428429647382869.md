## Hook and message
Single idea: a deadline. For people already considering Ascend who need a push. Promise: join the founding cohort, and get early bird terms if you apply by 26 November 2026.

## Claims and proof
- 'Early Bird Applications Closing on 26 November 2026'. A dated deadline. The image does not say what the early bird benefit is.
- 'Founding Online PGP Cohort'. Confirms a first cohort.
- Body: '12-month', 'Weekends, about 10 hours a week, and you keep your job'. Programme facts.
- No scholarship amount in this ad's body, unlike the plain founding cohort ads.

## Offer and call to action
Early bird deadline 26 November 2026. The benefit of being early is not stated in this ad. Button on image: Apply Now. Ad button: Apply now. Body ends 'Apply before the deadline.'

## Why it may work
- The date is on the image, underlined, so urgency lands without reading the body.
- Started 17 Nov for a 26 Nov deadline, so it is a nine day countdown push.
- Simple and fast. Works well as retargeting for people who saw the longer ads.

## Where it is weak
- It never says what early bird gets you. A deadline with no reward is weak pressure.
- No reason to want the programme for anyone seeing Mesa for the first time.
- The blurred background adds nothing and makes the card look unfinished next to the clean flat version.
