Apply to the Online PGP in Business, AI & Entrepreneurship (Ascend). No price, scholarship or deadline in this ad. Button on the image: Apply Now. Ad button: Apply now.

## Why it may work
- It removes the biggest objection (quitting) in the headline.
- Bold white type on black is the most legible version of this message, and the gold panel puts the button mid-image.
- The body gives a precise time cost, about 10 hours a week.

## Where it is weak
- No proof, number or outcome on the image.
- The photo shows two colleagues talking. It does not show a business being built.
- 'About 10 hours a week' is only in the body text.
