## Hook and message

You can make real money fast by building and selling a product. It is for working people who want proof that they can sell something. The promise is ₹5 lakh generated in 8 weeks.

## Claims and proof

- 'Generate 5L in just 8 Weeks'. No source, no footnote, no 'up to' or 'students have'. It reads as a promise of revenue.
- The slide in the photo shows a real student brand pitch (HOOVU, a fragrance product). This is soft proof that students build products, but it does not back the 5L number.
- 'Online', '12 Months'. Programme facts, no source needed.

- Button on the image: Apply Now.

- Destination: https://mesaschool.co/ascend/

## Why it may work / where it is weak

Why it may work:

- A hard rupee number with a short time frame stops the scroll.
- The photo shows a real pitch with a real pricing slide, so the build claim feels concrete.
- Clean hierarchy: number, photo, programme, button.

Where it is weak:

- The 5L claim has no source and no qualifier. It is the kind of line a regulator or a sceptical reader will challenge.
- 'Generate' is vague. It does not say revenue, profit or team total.
