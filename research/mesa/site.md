## How the ads differ from the site
- The site leads with outcome numbers; most Mesa video ads do not. Outcome figures appear in about one in four full-time PGP ads (see the comparison page), and they disagree with each other (100 / 150 / 500 startups; 25 lakh average salary against 2.8x and 50 to 60% rises).
- Ascend ads repeat the site's "keep your job" promise almost word for word in image headlines; the Ascend speech never says "Ascend".
- No sampled ad states a fee. Ascend image ads name an early bird close (26 November 2026 on one idea) while the site says 29 Nov 2026.
- "MBA" appears mainly as the thing Mesa is not ("Not another MBA" image ads).
