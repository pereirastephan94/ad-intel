# Handover to the SSB PGP chief of staff: Mesa ad library research, version 1 (19 Nov)

For the Head of Copy and the performance copy bot. It fills the gap in the existing Mesa brief: pacing, the first 3 seconds, and music.

## Where the full work is

- Files: this repo, branch `claude/yesterday-cpls-TCJO8`, folder `research/mesa/`. Start with `doc/patterns.md`, `doc/ssb-insights.md`, `doc/summary.md`. Per video: `videos/<id>/breakdown.md`. Per image ad: `statics/<id>.md`.
- Coverage: 241 of roughly 310 live ads (44 distinct videos, 115 image ads); the pull is still running. Spend and results are not public, so this shows what Mesa is betting on, not what is winning. The figures below were counted on the first 163 ads (35 videos, 71 image ads).
- New in ads 164 to 241: a named co-founder interview ("Who is Mesa for?" - "Well, it's not for everyone") with a 23 s music-only montage of headline cards and school logos; an age call-out ("If you are 25 years old, the worst thing you can do is work in a back-end role in a large MNC", 214 wpm); a reaction format (borrowed stage clip, then selfie "Finally, somebody said it"); new image themes for Ascend ("Learn business from top faculty and the founders who've done it", "You've mastered your role, now lead the whole business", "Career coach from month one, and a network of founders you keep for life") and PGP ("The only B-school where your startup is the syllabus"). Everything else is a repeat or re-cut.

## The shape of Mesa's account
- A launch ramp: 114 of 163 ads started 16 to 18 Nov. About half the ads, and 53 of 71 image ads, sell Ascend (part-time, ages 25 to 38, "keep your job"). Ascend has no graduates; its proof is borrowed from the full-time PGP. Its own ads disagree on "Online" vs "Hybrid" and on early bird closing 26 or 29 Nov.
- One video format: a person talking in a Mesa office or on a podcast set. 35 files are about 22 distinct edits. Families: 13 interview cuts (two shoots), 9 staff talking heads, 6 undergraduate, 3 investor-style endorsements, 2 borrowed clip plus selfie, 2 student story.
- Most deployed PGP video: "We are looking for 200 special people" (38 s, one man, no b-roll, no proof): 7 ads, 13 copies.

## First 3 seconds (all 35 videos)
- Hook type: 12 statistic, 8 story, 4 category problem, 3 curiosity tease, 2 question. 9 name the audience in the first sentence.
- 32 of 35 open on the speaker mid-word and mid-gesture. None opens on a logo or title.
- Text on screen by 0.5 s in 30 of 35. A number spoken or shown within 3 s in 18. About 20 use a coloured stat card in that window.
- Movement in second one is a zoom, not a cut. 14 of 35 have no real cut in the first 5 s.
- Logo in a corner from frame one in 29 of 35. Mesa is spoken in the first sentence in 4, after 20 s in at least 10, never in 7.
- 5 open with "You know,". Mesa also runs one of those with the filler cut, so they are testing it.
- Strongest opening lines: "Here's a stat most business schools will never publish." / "In the first eight weeks of their curriculum, every student has to sell 5 lakhs worth of product." / "I spent two years preparing for UPSC. Today, I work directly with the founder of a fast-growing F&B startup." / "One of the questions in CAT 2025 was this." / "So should I tell you the number one mistake that mid-career working professionals make?" / "I know somebody who 5x-ed the profits of his family business by using AI."

## Pacing and sound
- Median video: 60.8 s, 171 words per minute, 3.28 cuts per 10 s, 179 words (range 96 to 329).
- Median cuts per 10 s / words per minute: PGP 3.03 / 171; Ascend 3.40 / 167; Undergraduate 5.25 / 183.
- Slow end: direct-to-lens talking heads at 1.0 to 2.6 cuts per 10 s, no b-roll. Fast end: 6+ cuts per 10 s, one b-roll shot per phrase with a stat card on most beats.
- Speech starts at 0 s and fills 82 to 98% of the runtime. The only regular silence is a 4.5 to 7 s silent black end card.
- Music is measured by software, not heard. About half the videos seem to have a low bed under the voice; tempo guess clusters near 123 bpm. Tracks cannot be named. Voice and captions do the work.

## Look
- Polo in 23 of 35, casual shirt in 10, no suits. Only 6 wear Mesa-branded clothing. 20 of 35 never look at the lens.
- Gold for Ascend, green for PGP, crimson for Undergraduate. Word-level burned-in captions. Only 16 of 35 are true vertical; Ascend has no vertical video.

## Script structure
- Arcs: outcome, mechanism, more proof (10); problem, then "that's where Mesa comes in" (9); student result, then "every student does this" (6); advice with no pitch (4); third-party endorsement (3); direct pitch (3).
- One mechanism repeats: sell ₹5 lakh of product in the first 8 weeks. AI is in 24 of 35. 12 of 35 define Mesa against other schools.

## Mesa's weak spots
1. 22 of 35 have no spoken call to action. No video states a fee, deadline or next step.
2. Speakers named on screen in 2 of 35; no job title ever. Only one student speaks.
3. No number is sourced, and figures conflict: partner startups are 100, 150 and 500; one student brand earns 20 and 25 lakh a month; salary is "25 lakh", "50 to 60%" and "2.8x".
4. Proof is told, not shown; some b-roll contradicts the line.
5. Two shared headlines sit on 26 video ads and match almost none of the videos.
6. 4 of 6 Ascend videos never say the school or product aloud.

## Brief for the Head of Copy
Checked against SSB's script rules (7 beats, one number every 25 to 31 words, named companies, one call to action with seats, deadline and scholarship).
- **Borrow:** the plain 35 to 45 s talking head; text by 0.5 s and a number by 3 s; one audience per video, named in line one; one shoot planned as 8 to 10 standalone answers; casual clothing.
- **Beat them on:** source on screen beside every number; speaker name and title in the first 5 s; show the named company project and the founder in the room; a spoken call to action with a date; one in three videos led by a student; vertical first.
- **Avoid:** zoom-only openings and "You know,"; advice videos that never name SSB; attacking the MBA head-on; unsourced big numbers.
- **First test:** six 35 to 45 s vertical talking heads, two student-led project videos, one 250-word 7-beat video for warm audiences, and image ads with the deadline and scholarship on the image.
