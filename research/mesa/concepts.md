# Mesa School of Business: video concepts

126 video ads on 44 video assets fall into 30 concepts. Counts are ads in ads.json whose `video_asset` equals the asset id; a concept's count is the sum over its assets, and the counts add up to 126 (4 + 7 + 4 + 1 + 3 + 4 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 5 + 3 + 1 + 6 + 1 + 4 + 6 + 1 + 6 + 8 + 6 + 3 + 4 + 9 + 4 + 10 + 4 + 1 + 1 + 7 + 1). Image ads are not covered here. Rules used: the same footage in another shape, length or with another end card is one concept (c01, c02, c06, c08, c09, c10, c19, c20, c21, c23). The same interview session cut into different answers is a different concept each time (see the Ascend shoot below). The same script with a different person would be a new concept with `script_twin` set; no such pair exists at Mesa, because the same few people speak every script. Where one recording is re-cut with a different opening sentence (the investor, c11 and c19) the hook differs, so the two cuts are separate concepts linked by `script_twin`.

All 44 videos are person-led: 20 are V1 (a person speaking to the lens) and 24 are V2 (an interviewee answering an off-camera voice, or a podcast set). There is no V3 or V4 video. Row and face follow the review rulings: an off-camera interviewee is V2, and an unlabelled Mesa speaker is tagged `presenter` (not `actor_model`; see the tagger's notes).

## Series and recurring devices

- **The Ascend shoot, cut into six ideas.** One interview session (maroon polo, dark armchair, teal wall, wooden "m" sign) gives c03, c13, c15, c16, c18 and c20 (32 ads, with the PGP end-card cut of c20 included). They are different ideas because each is a different answer from the same sitting. c03 (#1 mistake) and c16 (90%) overlap most: both open on mid-career professionals going too narrow and both say "27 to 32 year olds". Each has its own headline and text that names Ascend and "12 months of weekends", which the speech never does.
- **Advice list, then a silent pitch.** c03, c16 and c20 end on a numbered list (business basics, build something, network or learn AI) with no spoken Mesa pitch at all; the ad text and end card do the selling.
- **One investor recording, two edits.** c11 opens on "I've funded 200 plus startups"; c19 opens on the 5 lakh line (both cuts, 60 s and 90 s). Same man, same podcast set, same body script, linked by `script_twin`. The 8-week 5 lakh line is said in c11, c19 and c26.
- **The borrowed-clip selfie.** c02 is one 105 second video in four shapes (4:5, 9:16, 1:1 and a card-in-frame re-upload), 7 ads, over two different ad texts ("The next decade belongs to builders" and "Your classmates could be your co-founders").
- **The 200-person cohort call, two scripts.** c23 (v1, "200 special people", fifth cohort) and c01 (v2, "200 people who do not want to learn business the traditional way") share the speaker, room and card style but not the script; they are separate ideas. The 13 ads whose text opens "Mesa is selecting 200 ambitious individuals" sit over four videos: both c23 videos (9 ads), the c07 interview (3 ads, 1290720899885510, 1801699367686717, 1797020924926579) and the c14 interview (4788179028135367). c01 carries its own text ("200 people. 12 months. 2000+ hours").
- **Careers-lead interview, three cuts.** c06 is one interview in three versions (vertical without opener, square and vertical with "You know, people ask me"). The cuts with the opener carry the filler "You know," that also opens c22, c24 and c26. c30 uses the same interview setup, shirt and caption design as the c06 vertical cut (per its breakdown).
- **Undergraduate podcast-mic set.** c22, c24 and c29 are one speaker at a studio microphone, cutting to student photos, product shots and a company logo card. c05, c17 and c25 are the same school's to-lens presenters. Undergraduate students are talked about in all six UG videos and speak in none.
- **The Capture app twice.** The same student app appears in c05 (as a Venture Building pitch to Elevation) and c22 (as an App Store launch); c05 shows a Mesa prize cheque under "pitched to Elevation Capital".
- **Shark Tank and the khakra brand.** c24 and c25 both tell the khakra story; c18, c25 and c26 each cite Shark Tank ("Sharp Tank" in the c18 transcript). The same student results are reused across PGP, Ascend and Undergraduate videos.
- **Name cards.** Only two Mesa speakers are named on screen: Ankit Agarwal, "Harvard Business School / MBA, Class of 2016" (c27 and the v2 cut of c08, 1846255523166302) and Varun Limaye, "Co-founder, Mesa" (c28, 4332149430419153). The only tagged `founder_declared` face is c28. The investor in c11 and c19 is never named on screen; his endorser mark is "AV" and the link label carries a name, which is not on the ad itself.
- **Silent black end card.** Every video ends on the same black card with the programme name and an "Apply Now" button (PGP white and green, Ascend gold, Undergraduate crimson with "On-campus | Bangalore"). c13 ("Stuck in a rut") ends without a captured end card.
- **Headlines shared across unrelated videos.** "The only B-school teaching AI agents & no-code" sits on 18 ads over 8 videos (c04, c08 twice, c11, c12, c19 twice, c27: the 25-year-old, CAT, investor, stat and MAT videos) and "Your classmates could be your co-founders" on 16 ads over 8 videos (c02, c06 three times, c09 twice, c26, c30); neither is said in the video. The Ascend ads are the exception: each headline matches its video.
- **No ad text.** 15 video ads have no body and no headline, all Undergraduate: c05 (3), c17 (1), c22 (1), c24 (3), c25 (3), c29 (4). `ad_text_missing` is set only where every ad of an asset is empty (c05 and c17), so the other four assets were tagged with their partial text.
- **The weakest hooks open on a filler.** "You know," opens c06 (two cuts), c22, c24 and c26.

## Numbers that disagree across ads

Library ids are the first ad of the video named; every ad on the same asset repeats the claim.

- **Startups that hire from Mesa: 100, 150, 500.** c30 (997098783438393 asset, ad 40210023251944395) says "more than 100 plus startups trust talent from Mesa". c11 and c19 say "150 plus startups are already hiring from there now" (4585145681703758, 4714358782134470, 1652092446560613). c06 says the careers team is "in touch with at least 500 startups" (1498875895417529, 1892035724994340, 1038867278612663). The ad text of c12 (1634433821103521) adds "100+ live startups, founders and VCs" in Bengaluru, a fourth framing.
- **One student brand at 20 and 25 lakh a month.** c24 says three 17 year olds built a khakra brand "that does 20 lakh in revenue every single month" (1885584672793111); c25 says "three students who have built a Khakra brand doing 25 lakh in revenue every single month" (1512248814079802). The two ads started on the same day.
- **Salary claims.** c09 says "average salary post program is upwards of 25 lakhs" (1488536809855650, 958469737116105) with no definition; c30 says alumni get "a 50 to 60% increase in salaries one year into the job" (40210023251944395). A 25 lakh average and a 50 to 60% rise cannot be checked against each other, and the placements cuts add "100% placements" (c06, 1498875895417529) beside "57.5% joined super early stage startups" (c12, 1634433821103521) and "almost 40% of our students get jobs through" founders (c07, 1290720899885510).
- **Revenue per student against per business against combined.** The 8-week line is "every student has to sell 5 lakhs worth of product" (c11, c19: 4585145681703758, 1652092446560613), "every single student does 5 lakh in sales" (c26, 1576676950895208), "generate over 5 lakh in revenue" from a D2C business (c01, 1609473000417031), "real revenue of more than 5 lakhs" (c04, 29250519677942503), and "reaching revenues of almost one crore" for each student business (c02, 1358399446249615). The scope (per student, per team, per cohort) is not stated in any of them.
- **Founders on the faculty or visiting.** c01 says "200 plus founders and operators" (1609473000417031); c04 says "more than 200 founders, operators and VCs" (29250519677942503); c07 says "100 plus founders" (1290720899885510) and c12 says "100 plus founders visit our campus" (1634433821103521).
- **Careers team size.** c06 is read in three cuts as a 15, 55 or 60-person team (a blurred green number; the transcript says "60%"): 1498875895417529, 1892035724994340, 1038867278612663. The number does not land on screen.
- **Student startup track record: 50 crore, 25 crore, Shark Tank.** c26 says "50 crore plus in ARR" (1576676950895208) and c18 says "almost 50 crores" (1130619536495111); both add 25 crore raised and Shark Tank. c18 and c26 use the number to sell the Ascend and full-time PGP respectively, though the track record is from the full-time students. c25 gives one Shark Tank student and a 2 crore grant (1512248814079802); c26 says two students have been on Shark Tank (1576676950895208).
- **Pitches to VCs.** c26 promises "demo days where you get to pitch to 50 plus VCs" (1576676950895208); c21 says a student "pitched it to 50 plus VCs, scaled it to 1000 plus monthly active users" (1599280982005523, 1450599547178860, 1130359959636135). The Capture app in c22 has "thousands of users" (983620227695155) beside a store page the breakdown reads as 4 ratings.
- **Cohort wording.** c23 says "the fifth cohort" (1518020923712110, 2199957264231150) and the c11 and c19 ad text says "Applications open for the 5th cohort", while the c02 ad text says "Cohort 2028 applications are open" (1358399446249615).
- **Rhetorical figures stated as fact.** "Almost 95% of jobs in MNCs" and an average company of 30 employees (c02), "90% of mid-career professionals" and "100% going to become irrelevant" (c16, 1660152285750346), "99.9% of companies fail in the mind" (c18, 1130619536495111), "only 0.001% of people" (c21) and "less than 5% of applicants get an offer" (c25, 1512248814079802) carry no source or definition in any video.
- **Programme named differently across the same shoot.** The c20 interview is Ascend in 2034753867202742 (landing page `/ascend`) and a full-time PGP in 2224966578082934 (homepage), with the same speech and a different end card. The Ascend speech never says "Ascend"; it says "the hybrid PGP in business, AI and entrepreneurship".
