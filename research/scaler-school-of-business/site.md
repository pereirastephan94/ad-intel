## How the ads differ from the site
- **No outcome figure reaches the ads.** The site leads with 19 and 23 LPA average CTC by cohort, 3.2x salary hike, 100% placement rate, 68% career pivot, 1,200+ hiring partners and internship stipends; none of these appears in the text, script or end card of any of the 30 sampled ads.
- **The call to action is thinner than the site's.** The site states an early bird close (29 Nov 2026), scholarships up to 100% and weekly admission phases; no sampled ad names a scholarship or a seat count, and the only deadline in the ads is "1st November 2026", on 4 ads started 16 to 18 days after it.
- **"MBA" against "certificate".** 29 ad texts say "MBA-equivalent PGP" and 22 videos say "MBA" aloud (5 call SSB's own students MBA students); the site says the programme is a certificate, not a degree, and SSB is not a university.
- **Tagline wording.** Ads: "India's Only B-School Designed by Industry Leaders" (18 headlines). Home: "India's only B-school built by industry leaders for India's future leaders". Landing page news card: "India's first business school built by industry leaders". One end card: "India's First Industry and AI-Led Business School".
- **Programme length.** End cards: "18-Month"; the timeline ads promise results "by month 3" and "in less than a year", which the site's terms (5 terms, then internship) do not contradict but do not state.
- **"50 lakh".** Ads use it three ways; the site's only 50 lakh is the curriculum video title above (a dropshipping challenge), and its D2C target is "₹5L+ revenue within 6 weeks".
- **Working professionals.** Scaler's own online PGP promises "Upskill without having to quit your job" and "Weekends only", the line Mesa's Ascend ads lead on; no sampled SSB ad is for it.
