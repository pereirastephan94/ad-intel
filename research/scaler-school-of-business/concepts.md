# Scaler School of Business: concepts

30 ads (all video, no image ads) on 16 video assets fall into 11 concepts. Counts are ads in ads.json whose `video_asset` equals the asset id. A concept's count is the sum over its assets, and the counts add up to 30, the number of ads in ads.json (3 + 2 + 2 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 3 + 5 + 3). Rules: the same footage in another shape or length is one concept (c02, c03, c05, c10), and so is the same body script with a different first sentence from the same person (c10). The same interview source cut into a new script, or told about a new subject, is a new concept (c03, c05 and c08 share a closing line but not a hook). Concepts are numbered in the order of the video folders. Concentration is moderate: the largest idea carries 5 of the 30 ads and the three largest carry 12.

## Series and recurring devices

- **One page, one button**: all 30 ads say "Apply now" and go to scaler.com/school-of-business/minimal-landing/. They carry 45 copies in all, on Facebook and Instagram. 25 of the 30 started between 17 and 19 Nov (6 on 17 Nov, 5 on 18 Nov, 14 on 19 Nov); the other five started on 6 Nov (2, c02) and 13 Nov (3, c10).
- **Shared ad text**: 27 ads carry "At SSB, business isn't taught in theory ... An MBA-equivalent PGP built for how business ..." in the main text and "At Scaler School of Business, students don't wait for placements to learn business ..." as the link description. Two ads (c04: 1537759174845618, 30428598886741065) swap the two texts, and one (c07: 4572207989702796) has its own text, "Experience an immersive PGP with hands-on learning ... Apply now!". The second link description, "admissions aren't about perfect scores", sits on 8 ads (c10 5, c11 3).
- **Headline set**: "India's Only B-School Designed by Industry Leaders" on 18 ads, a cut-off "... Designed by Industry" on 3 (2673709099725314, 2266227914289675, 2220191705516119), no headline on 6, "Early Bird Deadline - 1st November 2026" on 2 (954275137769376, 1123141853818596) and "Learn business by building one" on 1 (4572207989702796). The Industry Leaders headline is not said in any of the 16 videos.
- **End card template**: 22 ads end on the SSB logo, "PGP in Management & Tech", "Apply Now", "18-Month | Full-Time | Bangalore Campus". Two (c07) have a different card ("India's First Industry and AI-Led Business School", the early bird date, "18-Month Full-Time PG Program in Management & Technology"). Six (c04 4, c06 2) end on the logo alone.
- **Same footage, new shape**: c02 (16:9 and 9:16), c03 (16:9 and 4:5), c05 (16:9 and 4:5) and c10 (square and 16:9). The sample holds all four shapes: square 13 ads, 16:9 5, 9:16 10, 4:5 2.
- **Interview source reused**: the closing sentence "If I was a student ... like an SSB today, I do think that exposure would have helped" is spoken in five videos, c03 (2 videos), c05 (2) and c08 (1), six ads, in three different scripts. None of the three labels who speaks.
- **Openings that run down the MBA** (checked in the hook text and first sentence of every video): 3 ads (c01, 1520277566815599, 1550037320477300, 2584177385340569) open on "outdated" and "unemployable", the latter with the unsourced "one in four". Counting every opener that calls the MBA or college outdated, dead, not enough or its graduates underused, the total is 11 of 30 ads: c01 (3), c04 (4), c05 (3) and the 4:5 cut of c03 (1150917287446121). A further 4 ads (2559185667923714, 3476645972514831, 1702071564771479 and 2160336601279943, all c10) open by comparing SSB with "most MBA graduates". The same ads sell an "MBA-equivalent PGP", and 5 ads call SSB's own people MBA students or an MBA college aloud (c08 1, c10 1, c11 3).
- **Spoken school name** (video transcripts checked for "Scaler School of Business", "SSB" and mis-hearings of both, then against the breakdowns): the full name is spoken in 5 videos and 12 ads (c01, c02 both cuts, c04, c09); only "SSB" in 10 videos and 16 ads (c03, c05, c07, c08, c10, c11); never in 1 video and 2 ads (c06, where the school appears only as logos on banners and the end card). First spoken inside the first 6 s in c07, c08, c10 and c11, at about 18 s in c09, 28 s in c01, and between 32 and 41 s in c02, c03, c04 and c05. "Scaler" alone is spoken in c02 and c07.
- **Programme never spoken**: "PGP" is not spoken in any of the 16 videos. It appears in the ad text of all 30 ads and on the end card of 22 (see Products advertised).
- **How speakers are labelled**: no ad labels anyone as SSB faculty, student, founder or alumnus. Role caption: 4 ads (c04, "Zerodha Co-founder Nikhil Kamath", a role at another company, given for the quoted man only). Name only, no role: 8 ads (c10, "STEPHAN PEREIRA" in 5; c11, "Anupam Mittal" then "Anupam Mittal at SSB" in 3). No label at all: 18 ads (c01, c02, c03, c05, c06, c07, c08, c09). Unlabelled on-screen names that the ads do not tie to the speaker: a banner "IN CONVERSATION WITH SHANTANU DESHPANDE" in c03 and c08 b-roll, a desk card "Harsh" in c04, a caption "The Whole Truth" in c07. Tags give face `presenter` for 21 ads, `actor_model` 3, `customer` 2 and `celebrity_named` 4; only c04's evidence is a caption.
- **Image ads**: none. All 30 sampled ads have media type VIDEO in ads.json, and the research folder has no statics folder or `.tags.json` image files. The sample is 30 of the 36 live ads on the page; the other six were not pulled.
- **Spoken call to action**: "Apply now" is spoken in 2 ads (c02). A soft ask is spoken in 7 more ("do consider Scaler School" in c01, "this is the right place" in c04). 21 ads have no spoken ask and rely on the end card or button.
- **Named third parties**: Mokobara is spoken or captioned in c02 (2 ads), c04 (4) and c07 (2); "Shark Tank" is spoken in c06 and c09 (5 ads); Sequoia, McKinsey, Zomato, Razorpay and Apple in c10 (5), spoken only, with no logos in the stills.
- **Intake and money figures**: "the next 100 students" is said only in c10 (5 ads). No fee, scholarship, seat count, placement or salary figure appears in any ad text, script or end card. The only money figures are 50 lakh (12 ads, three meanings, below) and 1.5 lakhs (c06, a vendor transfer).
- **Language**: 24 ads are English and 6 Hinglish (c09 3, c11 3).

## Numbers that disagree across ads

- **Early bird deadline already past**: 4 ads still show "Early Bird Deadline 1st November 2026" after it. The headline of 954275137769376 and 1123141853818596 (c05, 16:9 cut, started 19 Nov, 18 days after the date) and the end card of the c07 video under 1406583844890391 and 4572207989702796 (started 17 Nov, 16 days after). The c05 4:5 cut (1641897867684154, 18 Nov) has no headline and no date, and no other ad shows a deadline or a replacement date. No ad says what the early bird offer is.
- **50 lakh, three meanings**: a brand "scaling it to 50 lakh rupees" in the month-3 beat (caption on 2559185667923714, 2160336601279943), the same line heard by the machine as "50 lakh copies" in the "less than a year" cut (2128818651073533), "aiming to hit 50 lakhs in just 12 [?]" for D2C businesses (1171509055224316, 2220191705516119, 1537759174845618, 30428598886741065) and "offered 50 lakhs for his start up on campus" as funding (1124686257016435, 2072772623441868, 1868107184215645). It reads as revenue, units or investment, with no source or student named.
- **Length of the programme**: the end card says "18-Month" (22 ads), c10 promises results "in less than a year", "by month 3" and "in six months", and the c04 voiceover says "you've learned more about business in the last two years" (1171509055224316, 2220191705516119, 1537759174845618, 30428598886741065, whose end card names no length).
- **What the programme is called**: the voice says "MBA student" or "MBA graduates" (c10, c11, c01), the ad text says "MBA-equivalent PGP", the end card says "PGP in Management & Tech" and the c07 card says "PG Program in Management & Technology". The gate banner in c05 reads "Post Graduate Program in Management ...". No ad says what the PGP is equivalent to or whether it is a degree.
- **School tagline against the headline**: the headline "India's Only B-School Designed by Industry Leaders" (18 ads) is not said in any video. The c07 end card says "India's First Industry and AI-Led Business School", a wall in c01 reads "for the Industry Leaders of Tomorrow" and a poster in c05 reads "a program built by Industry Leaders for Future Leaders". "Only", "First" and the named leaders are never backed on screen.
- **Ad text against the video**: the text of 4572207989702796 promises to "pitch to VCs" and unlock placements; the c07 video shows no placement figure and no VC. 2171343337139409 says "every MBA student at SSB has built businesses ... and pitched to VCs", shown only as b-roll.
- **Unsourced macro figures**: "one in four MBA graduates is actually unemployable" (c01, 3 ads) and "1 million jobs a month ... at 100,000" (c02, 2 ads, caption "We're at 100,000") carry no source, date or on-screen proof.

## Products advertised

All 30 ads sell one programme, the full-time postgraduate programme in management and technology (18 months, Bangalore Campus, per the end cards). The ads never call it a degree. Buttons: "Apply now" on all 30, to one landing page. The split below is by the wording on the end card, one row per ad.

|---|---|---|

| "18-Month Full-Time PG Program in Management & Technology" | 2 | c07 (2) |
| No programme named (logo only) | 6 | c04 (4), c06 (2) |

22 + 2 + 6 = 30, the number of ads in ads.json.
