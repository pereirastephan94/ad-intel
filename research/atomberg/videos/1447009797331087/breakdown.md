## Why it may work, and where it is weak
Works:
- A familiar irritation (the yearly AMC visit) and a clear question about why every filter is changed.
- Captions make the first 20 s work on mute.
- A different angle (transparency and control) from the brand's "adaptive" and "2X water saving" lines.

Weak:
- The product shows only at 22 s and is not named on screen.
- The proof (app, price per filter) is spoken or in the ad text, never shown legibly.
- The opening footage looks generated, which may feel less real than the selfie.
- The second half is hard to check because the speech is garbled.
