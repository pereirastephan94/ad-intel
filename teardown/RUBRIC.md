# Rubric: is this comparison good enough to show a founder?

Version 1 (20 Nov). Score each line 0, 1 or 2. Ship at 17 of 20 or more, with no zero.
This file is meant to grow: when a review finds a miss the rubric did not catch, add a line or sharpen one.

| # | Test | 0 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 60-second read | Reader must scroll to find the verdict | First screen says who is ahead, on what, and the 3 moves to make |
| 2 | Evidence | Claims with no ad behind them | Every claim has a count (n of N) and a link or still |
| 3 | Gap framing | Describes each brand separately | "They do this, you do not" by lane: video, founder or face-led, image ads, offer, landing page |
| 4 | Action | Observations only | Each gap has a next move a team could brief tomorrow, with what to test first |
| 5 | Honest limits | Reads as if it knows what works | Says sample size, that spend and results are not public, and what stands in for them (days running, copies) |
| 6 | Visual proof | Text only | Side-by-side stills and simple count charts, readable on a phone |
| 7 | Craft detail | Summaries of ads | Exact hooks and scripts, and frame-by-frame for the 2 or 3 standout ads only |
| 8 | Not generic | Could be said of any brand ("use stronger hooks") | Each insight would be false for a different pair of brands |
| 9 | Length | One long scroll at one depth | One-screen verdict, then depth behind folds |
| 10 | Fair both ways | Only what Y lacks | Also what Y does better than X, so the reader trusts it |
