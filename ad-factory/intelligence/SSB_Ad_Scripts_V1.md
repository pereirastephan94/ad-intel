# Scaler School of Business — Ad Scripts V1
## Production-Ready | 26 Mar 2026
## Format: 30–60 sec video | Facebook/Instagram Reels

---
> **STRUCTURE GUIDE (apply to every script):**
> - Hook: 0–3 sec — stop the scroll. One punchy sentence. On camera or text overlay.
> - Bridge: 3–8 sec — connect to SSB
> - Body: 8–40 sec — specifics, proof, what they'll get
> - CTA: final 5 sec — one action only
> - Tone: direct, confident, zero fluff. No "immersive", no "holistic", no "world-class".
---

# VIDEO SPECS (for production team)

|---|---|
| Duration | 30–45 sec (test 60 sec for Scripts 04 + 08) |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 primary (Reels/Stories) + 1:1 cut for Feed |

| Captions | Always on — 85% watch without sound |
| On-screen text | Reinforce key numbers: ₹50K, 60 seats, 100%, 18 months |
| Thumbnail | First frame must be compelling — text overlay on face |
| CTA card | Last 5 seconds: URL + "Apply Now" on screen |
| Music | Subtle, non-distracting. Silence is also fine. |
| Branding | SSB logo appears at 5 sec, not 0 sec — hook first, brand second |
