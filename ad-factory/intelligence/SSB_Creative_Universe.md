> **Purpose of this document:**
> This is the ground truth for the AI judge agent. Every creative brief, every ad copy, every video script produced by SSB must be evaluated against the dimensions in this document. # DIMENSION 1 — ICP MATRIX
## Who We're Talking To

Every creative must be written for exactly ONE ICP. Not two. Not "both freshers and professionals." One.

---

### ICP-01: THE WAITING FRESHER
**Who:** Final-year student or <1 year out of college. Engineering, commerce, or humanities. Feels the placement rat race is wrong for them but doesn't know what else to do. Scrolling LinkedIn anxiously.
**Age:** 21–24
**Education:** BE/BTech/BCom/BBA, tier 2–3 college mostly
**Pain:** Everyone around them is "applying" and they hate it. They want to build something but have no idea where to start and no capital.
**Dream:** Build a company. Be a founder. Do something real before they're 30.
**Fear:** Wasting their 20s in an entry-level job doing PowerPoints for someone else.
**Language they use:** "I don't want a job." / "I want to do something of my own." / "I'm tired of preparing for placements."
**What stops them:** No capital. No network. No validation. Parents want "a safe job."
**Hook sensitivity:** Contrarian (you're different), funded capital, execution velocity
**Competitor targeting them:** Mesa Forge ("Most freshers chase placements")
**SSB's answer:** ₹50K capital + Day 1 building + 100% internship = safe path to startup life

---

### ICP-02: THE STUCK PROFESSIONAL
**Who:** 2–5 years into a corporate job. MNC, consulting, banking, or ops. Good at their job. Hates it. Knows they're building someone else's dream. Has savings. Has time to plan.
**Age:** 25–32
**Background:** Big 4 accounting, Deloitte/McKinsey/BCG analyst, bank associate, ops manager at e-commerce
**Pain:** Feels like they missed the startup train. Smart enough to know the MBA is overpriced. Scared that AI will make their current role obsolete.
**Dream:** Work at a high-growth startup in a role that matters. Or build their own thing with credibility.
**Fear:** Making the wrong bet with their savings. Leaving a "safe" job for something that doesn't work out.
**Language they use:** "I want to pivot." / "I want to work at a startup but I don't have product/growth experience." / "Is it too late?"
**What stops them:** ROI anxiety. Partner/spouse expectations. Sunk cost of current career path.
**Hook sensitivity:** Career pivot, anti-MBA, scarcity, alumni proof (someone like them who did it)
**Competitor targeting them:** Mesa PGP Startup Leadership ("30 students from MNC and consulting backgrounds"), MU PGP Rise ("From Manager to CXO")
**SSB's answer:** Named alumni pivot (Kritika: Finance → Growth), 18 months vs 2-year MBA, ₹50K + internship

---

### ICP-03: THE MBA CONSIDERER
**Who:** Actively preparing for CAT, GMAT, or applying to IIMs. Smart, analytical. Has bought into the MBA dream but has nagging doubts about cost and relevance.
**Age:** 24–30
**Background:** Engineers, finance grads, consultants. 2–4 years experience.
**Pain:** ₹20–30L for 2 years. Opportunity cost. Not sure if IIM brand pays off in a startup world.
**Dream:** Senior role at a great company. Or eventually founder. Status + substance.
**Fear:** IIM reject. Or worse: getting in and finding out it wasn't worth it.
**Language they use:** "Is MBA still worth it?" / "CAT vs work experience" / "Should I do an MBA or join a startup?"
**What stops them:** Uncertainty. MBA = known quantity. SSB = unfamiliar.
**Hook sensitivity:** Anti-MBA (comparative), AI-first curriculum, cost comparison, named mentors
**Competitor targeting them:** Mesa (doesn't touch this), MU (touches lightly), SSB Script B partially
**SSB's answer:** 18 months vs 2 years, same outcome, ₹50K capital, 3 AI products, Deepinder/Kunal/Binny vs theory-first faculty

---

### ICP-04: THE AI-ANXIOUS NON-TECH
**Who:** Working professional in a non-technical role — finance, HR, marketing, operations, sales. Reads about AI replacing jobs. Worried. Wants to learn but doesn't know where to start. Definitely not a coder.
**Age:** 24–35
**Background:** Finance analyst, marketing manager, HR, sales, operations
**Pain:** Feels left behind by the tech wave. "AI is for engineers, not me." Afraid their role will be automated.
**Dream:** Be the person who knows how to use AI in their domain — irreplaceable, not replaced.
**Fear:** Learning to code (they don't want to). Looking stupid. Choosing the wrong program.
**Language they use:** "Do I need to learn coding?" / "How do I use AI in my job?" / "I'm not technical but..."
**What stops them:** Self-doubt about technical ability. Unclear outcomes.
**Hook sensitivity:** AI fear reframe ("AI won't replace you"), curriculum specificity (no coding required), tool names, specific student examples
**Competitor targeting them:** Mesa ("The people scared of AI will get left behind"), MU Applied AI PGP
**SSB's answer:** 150 hrs AI, 25 tools, 3 products, no tech background needed

---

### ICP-05: THE ASPIRING FOUNDER
**Who:** Has been "working on an idea" for 1–3 years. Maybe has a side project. Reads startup content obsessively. Follows founders on Twitter. Has the energy but lacks structure, network, and capital.
**Age:** 22–32, mix of freshers and early professionals
**Background:** Diverse — engineers, commerce, even arts
**Pain:** Inertia. Has ideas but can't execute. Doesn't know how to get first customers. Lacks co-founder, mentors, and capital.
**Dream:** Be a founder. Raise money. Build something that matters.
**Fear:** Failing publicly. Running out of money. Not being taken seriously.
**Language they use:** "I have this idea..." / "How do I get started?" / "I want to raise funding."
**What stops them:** Structure and accountability. Execution velocity.
**Hook sensitivity:** Execution velocity, funded capital, VC access, peer network
**Competitor targeting them:** Mesa ("The difference between aspiring founders and actual founders is execution velocity")
**SSB's answer:** ₹50K day 1, pitch to VCs month 6, build 2 businesses, ₹10Cr revenue from students

---

### ICP-06: THE CAREER PIVOTER (DOMAIN SWITCHER)
**Who:** Has clear domain they want to exit (finance, ops, HR, traditional marketing) and a clear domain they want to enter (growth, product, strategy, founder's office). Needs a credential + proof of work to make the switch.
**Age:** 26–33
**Pain:** Can't get interviews in new domain without experience. Can't get experience without interviews. Classic catch-22.
**Dream:** A role that feels like it matters. Closer to product, closer to customers.
**Fear:** The pivot doesn't work. They leave the safety of their current role for nothing.
**Hook sensitivity:** Named alumni pivot stories (Kritika), 68% career pivot stat, internship proof
**Competitor targeting them:** MU PGP Rise, Masters Union (general management)
**SSB's answer:** Kritika from Finance → Growth. 68% pivot rate. Internship as proof-of-work bridge.

---

### ICP-07: THE PARENT
**Who:** Parent of a 20–25 year old fresher or early professional. Paying or co-deciding on education. Risk-averse. Wants credential + career outcome + safety.
**Age:** 45–58
**Pain:** "My child has a degree but no clear path." Worried about ROI on education spend.
**Dream:** Child has a prestigious, stable, well-paying career at a recognisable company.
**Fear:** Child wastes 18 months + tuition on something that doesn't lead anywhere.
**Hook sensitivity:** Placement stat (100%), named mentors (Deepinder/Kunal/Binny = trust), on-campus/residential (safety), prestigious companies hiring
**Competitor targeting them:** Mesa Summer School, MU Young Leaders Cohort
**SSB's answer:** 100% placement, 150+ companies, Bengaluru campus, Deepinder Goyal mentor

# DIMENSION 2 — CREATIVE BUCKETS
## What Topic the Ad Is About

Every creative belongs to exactly ONE primary bucket. Secondary bucket is optional for hybrids.

---

### BUCKET-01: SCARCITY + SELECTIVITY
**Core message:** Admission is earned, not bought. We choose you as much as you choose us.
**Hook formula:** "[Number] seats. [Criterion]. [Urgency]."
**Psychological mechanism:** Identity signal (being selected = you're special) + FOMO
**Funnel stage:** TOFU → Cold audience, desire creation
**Works best for ICP:** 02 (Stuck Pro), 03 (MBA Considerer), 05 (Aspiring Founder)
**SSB proof points to use:** 60 seats, Cohort 2, applications close [date]
**Mesa equivalent:** "Mesa is selecting 150 ambitious individuals" / "Limited to 75 seats"
**MU equivalent:** Multiple cohort launches with round-based fee escalation
**Example hook:** "SSB is selecting 60 people for Intake 2. Not 600. Sixty."
**Do NOT:** Exaggerate selectivity without a real number. Must have a real deadline.

---

### BUCKET-02: FUNDED STARTUP CAPITAL
**Core message:** We give you real money to build a real business. No other B-school in India does this.
**Hook formula:** "What if your B-school gave you money to start?" OR lead with the number.
**Psychological mechanism:** Unique mechanism — eliminates the "I don't have capital" objection
**Funnel stage:** TOFU → Awareness, MOFU → Consideration
**Works best for ICP:** 01 (Waiting Fresher), 05 (Aspiring Founder), 02 (Stuck Pro)
**SSB proof points:** ₹25K–₹50K day 1, up to ₹25L milestone-based, ₹10Cr+ student revenue
**Competitor gap:** NOBODY else in Indian edtech offers this. Use it hard.
**Example hook:** "What if your business school gave you money to start a business?" OR "₹50,000. Day 1. Yours."
**Do NOT:** Conflate with loans or stipends. Frame as capital, not scholarship.

---

### BUCKET-03: CONTRARIAN / PATTERN INTERRUPT
**Core message:** You are not like everyone else. And the path everyone else takes is wrong for you.
**Hook formula:** "Most [people in your situation] do X. But you want Y."
**Psychological mechanism:** Identity contrast + self-selection. Makes the viewer feel seen and different.
**Funnel stage:** TOFU → Cold audience, scroll-stopping
**Works best for ICP:** 01 (Waiting Fresher), 05 (Aspiring Founder)
**SSB proof points:** 68% career pivot, ₹10Cr student revenue, Day 1 building
**Mesa equivalent (their top performer):** "Most freshers chase placements, but you want to build businesses."
**Example hooks:**
- "Most freshers open Naukri. You opened this ad. That's already different."
- "Most people with your idea are still 'thinking about it' 3 years later."
- "Your peers are preparing for CAT. You're wondering if there's a better way."
**Do NOT:** Be preachy or superior. The tone is conspiratorial, not condescending.

---

### BUCKET-04: AI — FEAR REFRAME
**Core message:** AI is coming. The question isn't whether to learn it — it's who teaches you properly.
**Hook formula:** "AI won't replace you. Someone who uses it will." OR "The people scared of AI will get left behind."
**Psychological mechanism:** Fear activation → immediate relief through solution
**Funnel stage:** TOFU → Cold, high-intent audience
**Works best for ICP:** 04 (AI-Anxious Non-Tech), 02 (Stuck Pro)
**SSB proof points:** 150 hrs AI practice, 25 tools, 3 AI products shipped, no coding required
**Competitor equivalent:** Mesa ("The people scared of AI will get left behind"), MU Applied AI PGP
**SSB's unfair advantage:** 150 hrs is specific and provable. MU has the program. SSB has the depth.
**Example hooks:**
- "AI won't replace you. But someone who knows how to use it will."
- "150 hours of AI. Not a module. Not a workshop. 150 hours."
- "You don't need to learn to code. You need to learn to build with AI."
**Do NOT:** Promise coding skills or technical outcomes. This ICP doesn't want to code.

---

### BUCKET-05: NAMED MENTOR / INVESTOR PROOF
**Core message:** The people who built India's biggest companies are your mentors. Not academics.
**Hook formula:** Lead with the name. The name does the work.
**Psychological mechanism:** Authority transfer + aspiration (proximity to greatness)
**Funnel stage:** MOFU → Warm audience, trust building
**Works best for ICP:** 02 (Stuck Pro), 03 (MBA Considerer), 07 (Parent)
**SSB proof points:** Deepinder Goyal (Zomato), Kunal Shah (CRED), Binny Bansal (Flipkart), Vijay Shekhar Sharma (Paytm), IIM faculty
**Competitor gap:** Nobody in Indian edtech has these 3 names. This is SSB's nuclear weapon.
**Example hooks:**
- "Deepinder Goyal will review your business idea. At SSB."
- "The people who built Zomato, CRED, and Flipkart are your mentors for 18 months."
- "What would it be worth to have Kunal Shah in your corner?"
**Do NOT:** Name-drop without specificity. "Industry mentors" is worthless. Named is everything.

---

### BUCKET-06: ALUMNI TRANSFORMATION STORY
**Core message:** Someone exactly like you did this. Here's what happened to them.
**Hook formula:** "[Name] was [what they were]. 18 months later — [what they became]."
**Psychological mechanism:** Social proof + identification + hope. Most believable creative type.
**Funnel stage:** MOFU → Consideration, BOFU → Conversion
**Works best for ICP:** 02 (Stuck Pro), 06 (Career Pivoter), 01 (Waiting Fresher)
**SSB proof points:**
- Kritika Bhatia: Financial Analyst → Growth Marketing at Emergent Labs
- 68% career pivot rate
- 100% internship placement
- 150+ companies hiring
**Named alumni to shoot:** Kritika (Finance→Growth), Dhruvin, Aayush — each gets their own ad
**Competitor gap:** Mesa uses NO named alumni. MU has some. SSB can dominate this bucket.
**Example hooks:**
- "Kritika left finance. 18 months later she was running growth for a startup."
- "Dhruvin had never sold anything. 6 months at SSB, he closed ₹4L in his first D2C launch."
**Do NOT:** Use generic "our students" language. Always name the person.

---

### BUCKET-07: ANTI-MBA / INTELLIGENT ALTERNATIVE
**Core message:** The 2-year MBA is an expensive legacy product. SSB is what you actually need in 2026.
**Hook formula:** Comparative structure — MBA column vs SSB column, or direct contrast.
**Psychological mechanism:** Loss aversion (they're wasting 2 years + ₹30L) + rational reframe
**Funnel stage:** TOFU/MOFU → MBA-aware audience
**Works best for ICP:** 03 (MBA Considerer), 02 (Stuck Pro)
**SSB proof points:** 18 months vs 2 years, same salary outcomes, ₹50K capital (MBA gives none), real faculty vs theorists
**Competitor gap:** Neither Mesa nor MU makes this case explicitly. SSB owns this lane.
**Example hooks:**
- "Why we built an 18-month program instead of a 2-year MBA."
- "2 years. ₹28 lakhs. Case studies. vs 18 months. ₹50K to build with. Real businesses."
- "I'm going to tell you something most MBA programmes don't want you to know."
**Do NOT:** Bash IIMs aggressively. Tone is "different choice, here's why" — not "IIMs are bad."

---

### BUCKET-08: CURRICULUM SPECIFICITY / LEARNING BY DOING
**Core message:** You learn by doing real things — not reading about them. Here's exactly what you'll do.
**Hook formula:** Specific verbs + specific outcomes. "You'll sell. You'll build. You'll pitch."
**Psychological mechanism:** Specificity = believability. Vague benefits = distrust.
**Funnel stage:** MOFU → Consideration
**Works best for ICP:** All ICPs in consideration phase
**SSB proof points:** D2C challenge (selling on streets of Bangalore), VC pitch month 6, 2 businesses built, 3 AI products shipped, mandatory internship, IIM faculty for theory, operators for practice
**Competitor equivalent:** Mesa ("At Mesa, you don't just study sales skills. You practice them.")
**Example hooks:**
- "In week 2 at SSB, you're selling on the streets of Bangalore. Not in a classroom."
- "Month 1: Build an MVP. Month 6: Pitch to VCs. Month 12: Graduate with proof of work."
- "We don't teach you how to write a business plan. We make you run one."
**Do NOT:** Use words like "immersive," "holistic," or "experiential." Show, don't tell.

---

### BUCKET-09: EXECUTION VELOCITY / ASPIRING FOUNDER
**Core message:** The gap between aspiring and doing is structure. We provide the structure.
**Hook formula:** Provocative question about inertia. "How long have you been saying...?"
**Psychological mechanism:** Identity challenge. Forces viewer to confront their own inaction.
**Funnel stage:** TOFU → Aspiring founders who've been stuck
**Works best for ICP:** 05 (Aspiring Founder), 01 (Waiting Fresher)
**SSB proof points:** ₹50K day 1, real customers week 2, VC pitch month 6, ₹10Cr student revenue
**Mesa equivalent:** "The difference between aspiring founders and actual founders is execution velocity."
**Example hooks:**
- "How long have you been saying 'I want to start something'?"
- "The gap between aspiring founders and actual founders isn't ideas. It's structure."
- "You'll pitch to VCs in month 6. Ready or not."
**Do NOT:** Be preachy. The tone is a peer challenging them, not a teacher lecturing them.

---

### BUCKET-10: PLACEMENT + CAREER OUTCOMES
**Core message:** Here's exactly what you'll earn and where you'll work after SSB.
**Hook formula:** Lead with the number or the company. "100%. 150+ companies. ₹X LPA."
**Psychological mechanism:** Risk reduction. Clear ROI makes the decision easier.
**Funnel stage:** BOFU → High-intent prospects comparing options
**Works best for ICP:** 02 (Stuck Pro), 03 (MBA Considerer), 07 (Parent)
**SSB proof points:** 100% internship placement, 68% career pivot, 150+ companies hiring, [add salary data when available]
**Competitor gap:** MU publishes avg CTC ₹33L. SSB must publish its numbers. Without this, MU wins every comparison.
**Example hooks:**
- "100% of SSB students land internships before graduating."
- "68% of SSB graduates completely changed their career. Here's where they went."
- "150+ companies are actively hiring from SSB's cohort. Here are 10 of them."
**Do NOT:** Use this bucket without real, specific numbers. "Great placements" is worthless.

---

### BUCKET-11: CAMPUS LIFE / RESIDENTIAL EXPERIENCE
**Core message:** This isn't online. This is 18 months of living and building with your cohort.
**Hook formula:** Visceral scene from campus. Makes the viewer feel FOMO for a place.
**Psychological mechanism:** Belonging + community + physical reality (vs online programs)
**Funnel stage:** MOFU → Warm audience, differentiator vs online
**Works best for ICP:** 01 (Waiting Fresher), 05 (Aspiring Founder)
**SSB proof points:** Bengaluru campus, residential program, D2C challenge (street selling), cohort becomes co-founders/advisors
**Competitor equivalent:** MU Bharat (travel-based immersion), Mesa (residential cohort)
**Example hooks:**
- "In month 3, your cohort will sell coffee on the streets of Bangalore. Together."
- "Your peers at SSB won't just be classmates. They'll be your first customers, co-founders, and advisors."
**Do NOT:** Use stock footage of generic campuses. Must be real SSB campus footage.

---

### BUCKET-12: OBJECTION HANDLING (RETARGETING)
**Core message:** You've been thinking about it. Here are the honest answers to every doubt.
**Hook formula:** "If you're watching this, you've already looked at SSB. Here's what you're probably wondering."
**Psychological mechanism:** Addresses specific, known objections at the moment of consideration
**Funnel stage:** BOFU → Retargeting warm audience (website visitors, video viewers >50%)
**Works best for ICP:** All ICPs in late consideration
**SSB proof points:** Transparent answers to: "Is it a real degree?" / "What about IIM?" / "What if I'm a fresher?" / "How much does it cost?"
**Example hooks:**
- "You Looked at SSB. Here's what people usually ask."
- "Honest answers about SSB. No brochure language."
**Do NOT:** Use this in cold audiences. Only retargeting.

---

### BUCKET-13: PARENT-TARGETED
**Core message:** Your child could build real companies, mentored by the people who built India's biggest ones.
**Hook formula:** Open with the parent's anxiety. Then reframe with SSB's outcome.
**Psychological mechanism:** Parental aspiration + safety signals (placement, campus, named mentors)
**Funnel stage:** TOFU → Cold parent audience
**Works best for ICP:** 07 (Parent)
**SSB proof points:** Deepinder/Kunal/Binny mentors (trusted names), 100% placement, Bengaluru campus (on-site = safe), 18 months (shorter than MBA)
**Example hooks:**
- "Your child could be mentored by the founder of Zomato. At SSB."
- "The program mentored by Deepinder Goyal has 60 seats. One might be for your child."
**Do NOT:** Treat parents as secondary. They often control the decision budget.

# DIMENSION 9 — JUDGE RUBRIC
## How the AI Judge Scores Every Creative

Each creative is scored out of 100 across 8 dimensions. Pass threshold: 70/100. Auto-reject: below 50.

### Scoring Dimensions

**1. ICP Specificity (0–15 points)**
- 15: One specific ICP is clear from the first 3 seconds. Language matches that ICP's exact pain/dream.
- 10: ICP is implied but not precise.
- 5: Could be for multiple ICPs (vague).
- 0: Completely generic ("for anyone who wants to build").

**2. Hook Strength (0–20 points)**
- 20: Hook is one of the 10 defined hook types. Lands within 3 seconds. Would stop a scroll.
- 15: Hook is clear and relevant but not a proven formula.
- 10: Hook exists but takes > 3 seconds.
- 5: Starts with product description or generic statement.
- 0: No hook. Opens with brand/logo/generic welcome.

**3. Proof Point Usage (0–15 points)**
- 15: Uses a Tier 1 USP with exact numbers (₹50K, Deepinder Goyal, ₹10Cr, 150 hrs).
- 10: Uses a Tier 2 USP with specificity.
- 5: Uses a USP but vaguely ("great mentors," "real experience").
- 0: Zero proof points. Pure claims.

**4. Competitor Differentiation (0–10 points)**
- 10: Uses a USP that no competitor can match (Funded Capital, Deepinder/Kunal/Binny, Anti-MBA).
- 7: Uses a USP SSB does better than competitors.
- 3: Uses a USP that competitors also claim (learning by doing, placements).
- 0: Uses a claim competitors own more strongly than SSB.

**5. ICP × Bucket Alignment (0–15 points)**
- 15: ICP and Bucket are a ✅ or 🔴 combination from the Angle Matrix.
- 10: ICP and Bucket are a ⚠️ combination.
- 0: ICP and Bucket are an ❌ combination. Auto-flag for rejection.

**6. Call to Action Fit (0–10 points)**
- 10: CTA matches funnel stage. Cold audience → "Apply / Learn More." Warm → "Book a call." BOFU → "Apply before [date]."
- 5: CTA exists but doesn't match funnel stage.
- 0: No CTA or wrong CTA for stage.

**7. Tone Compliance (0–10 points)**
- 10: Tone matches the Tone Code for this Bucket + ICP combination. Zero banned words.
- 5: Mostly correct tone with 1–2 minor deviations.
- 0: Contains banned words or wildly wrong tone (e.g. T-03 Warm on a cold TOFU contrarian ad).

**8. Format Fit (0–5 points)**
- 5: Format specified and matches recommended format for this bucket.
- 0: No format specified or wrong format.

### Judge Decision Logic
- **Score 85–100:** Approve immediately. Priority production.
- **Score 70–84:** Approve with notes. Minor refinements before production.
- **Score 55–69:** Return for revision. Judge specifies which dimension failed.
- **Score 50–54:** Reject with detailed reasoning. Not producible as-is.
- **Score <50:** Auto-reject. Starts over.
- **ICP × Bucket = ❌:** Auto-reject regardless of other scores.
- **Zero proof points:** Cap score at 55 regardless of other dimensions.
- **Contains banned words:** Deduct 10 points from Hook score.
