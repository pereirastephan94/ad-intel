# PART 1: CREATIVE STRATEGY — SCRIPTS & HOOKS

## SSB's Entire Active Ad Copy (verbatim — both scripts)

**Script A (47 of 49 variants):**
> *"Experience an immersive PGP with hands-on learning. Gain real-world skills, pitch to VCs & unlock top placements. Apply now!"*

**Script B (2 of 49 variants — started 23 Mar 2026):**
> *"Choosing an MBA in an AI-First World? Hear from our Head of Admissions. Join our Live Admissions Info Session — Limited Seats. Register Now."*

### Diagnosis of Script A:
- **No hook.** Opens with "Experience an immersive PGP" — this is product description, not a hook. Nobody scrolling Instagram is waiting to hear about your PGP.
- **No identity trigger.** It doesn't speak to WHO this is for. A fresher? A consultant? A parent? Nobody knows.
- **Generic benefit stacking.** "hands-on learning... real-world skills... top placements" — every B-school says this. Zero differentiation.
- **No tension or problem.** No pain point identified, no contrast created.
- **CTA mismatch.** "Apply now!" on a cold audience is asking for marriage on the first date. Requires retargeting infrastructure that isn't there.

### Diagnosis of Script B (the only interesting one):
- **Has a hook.** "Choosing an MBA in an AI-First World?" — this creates a problem/tension
- **Has a format.** Info session = lower commitment CTA = smarter for cold audiences
- **BUT:** Only 2 variants. Not being scaled or tested.

- UTM structure: Not readable from Ad Library (wrapped in FB redirect)
- Inferred: Campaign-level UTMs per product line (PGP vs Forge vs Summer)

## SSB
- Destination: `scaler.com` (main domain — harder to attribution-track without UTMs)
- UTM structure: Not readable from Ad Library
- CTA: "Apply Now" (Script A) + "Register Now" (Script B)

### SSB's URL/UTM Gaps:
1. **No dedicated landing page** — sending traffic to `scaler.com` homepage mixes SSB intent with SST/Academy traffic, muddying attribution
2. **No sub-domain or path** — Mesa uses `mesaschool.co` as a clean, standalone URL; SSB should use `school.scaler.com` or a standalone `ssb.scaler.com`
3. **No UTM-per-hook-type** — if you don't track by hook, you can't know which creative bucket is driving applications
4. **Single landing page** — Mesa likely has separate LPs per product; SSB appears to send everything to the same page

### Recommended UTM structure for SSB:
```
utm_source=facebook
utm_medium=paid_social
utm_campaign=ssb_pgp_professionals_cohort2
utm_content=hook_antamba_fresher [hook type]
utm_term=video_v3 [which video variant]
```

# PART 6: SAAS-STRATEGY LENS — WHAT'S MISSING

Applying a SaaS demand generation framework to SSB's ad strategy:

## 1. TOFU (Top of Funnel) — BROKEN
- **Problem:** One generic ad for all cold audiences
- **Fix:** 6–8 distinct hook buckets, each targeting a specific ICP's pain point
- **Mesa does:** Pattern interrupt ("Most freshers chase placements"), fear ("People scared of AI"), speed ("Fastest way to learn")

## 2. MOFU (Middle of Funnel) — ABSENT
- **Problem:** No retargeting-specific creative visible in Ad Library
- **Fix:** Separate campaign for warm audiences (website visitors, video viewers) with:
  - Objection-handling scripts ("Is SSB worth it? Here's what a student said honestly")
  - Alumni story ads (Kritika: Finance → Growth Marketing)
  - Proof-point ads (₹16L revenue, 100% placement, 68% career pivot)
- **Mesa does:** Running separate warm-audience ads with outcome promises

## 3. BOFU (Bottom of Funnel) — WEAK
- **Problem:** "Apply Now" on a cold audience with no trust built = low conversion
- **Fix:** Event/webinar ads (SSB does have Script B — needs to be scaled 10x)
- **Mesa does:** "Apply to Mesa's PGP in Startup Leadership" — but they build enough trust in the ad copy to earn the click

## 4. CREATIVE TESTING — NOT HAPPENING
- **Problem:** 47 variants of the same script = testing videos, not ideas
- **Fix:** Test the IDEA first (hook), then test the creative (video)
- **Mesa does:** 9 distinct ideas × 4–8 video variants each = systematic testing

## 5. PRODUCT LINE SEGMENTATION — MISSING
- **Problem:** SSB only advertises 1 product (PGP-MT)
- **Fix:** Does SSB have any short programs, events, or bootcamps? Advertise those. Lower commitment entry points build the funnel.
- **Mesa does:** PGP (professionals) + Forge (freshers) + Summer School (teens/parents) = 3 separate funnels

## 6. SOCIAL PROOF — DORMANT
- **Problem:** SSB has incredible social proof (Deepinder, Kunal, Binny, 100% placement) and uses none of it in ads
- **Fix:** Dedicated social proof ad bucket — name-drop every famous mentor, show Kritika's career pivot on camera, show the ₹16L revenue number in the first 3 seconds
- **Mesa does:** "taught by founders from Zomato, Swiggy, CRED" — but SSB has NAMED founders who are more famous
