Your path

3 career pathways. One programme.

Path 01

Own Startup

You build four businesses in four years at Scaler’s innovation lab with a seed funding pool of 5cr+, AI tools and SST tech co-founders, and pitch to 50+ investors at VC Demo Day.

Path 02

Family Business

You take electives in succession, working capital and exports, learn from mentors who run family businesses, and work on a live project to modernise and scale your family business in Year 4.

Path 03

Placements

You build your profile through 3 internships and live projects with brands like Ajio and Mokobara, get career prep in Year 3, and land a PPO or an on-campus offer from 1,200+ hiring partners.
