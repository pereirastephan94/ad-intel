FAQs

Questions, answered. For students and parents.

Who can apply?+

Students currently in Class XII, or who passed Class XII in 2026, with a minimum of 60% in Class XII from a recognised board (CBSE, ISC, state boards, IB, IGCSE or equivalent). The programme is open to all streams: science, commerce and humanities.

What does the admission process look like?+

Five steps: a mandatory 30-minute information session, the 70-minute online SSB Scholarship & Aptitude Test (SSAT), an online application with a video essay, two interview rounds, and the admission decision.

Can I skip the SSAT?+

Yes. Candidates with qualifying JEE, SAT, CUET or IPMAT scores may skip the SSAT and proceed directly to the application.

What is the application fee?+

The application fee is ₹1,000, paid when you submit your online application.

How much are the fees?+

The total programme fee is ₹39.5 lakh over four years: a ₹50,000 admission fee to confirm your seat, plus tuition fees payable term-wise. Hostel is optional, and the full fee structure is on the Admission & Fees page.

Are scholarships available?+

Yes. Scholarships are awarded based on SSAT performance, interviews and the overall profile, and all applicants are considered automatically. They include Merit, Builder, Leadership, Sports, Women in Business, Merit-cum-Need and Early Applicant scholarships.

When does the programme start, and where?+

The first batch starts in August 2027, on the Scaler campus in Bengaluru. There are only 100 seats.

When is the Early Round deadline?+

31st December 2026, 11:59 PM. The early-applicant scholarship is available only in the Early Round.
