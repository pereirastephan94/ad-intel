Curriculum

Curriculum that goes beyond classrooms.

Start with foundations to graduate into 3 career pathways: your own startup, a campus placement or your family business.

Year 1

Foundational Learning

Understand how businesses and AI work and apply the fundamentals by launching your first business and acquiring real customers.

Term 1

August to December

You learn to turn a problem into a product people buy.

Term project
MicroSaaS Tool, built in teams of three on no-code platforms
Beyond the classroom
Orientation Week street-selling challenge in Bengaluru
In class
Critical Thinking & Problem Structuring · Principles of Microeconomics · Business Communication, Storytelling & Digital Presence · Business Statistics · Introduction to Entrepreneurship · AI Foundations & No-Code Automation · Excel & PowerPoint Foundations
Immersions
Small business and markets (Chickpet, SP Road, Yeshwanthpur APMC) · Winter trek

Term 2

January to May

You learn to launch and run your own D2C brand.

Term project
D2C Brand Launch, with ₹50,000 in seed funding per team
Beyond the classroom
AI Product 1, a WhatsApp Customer Assistant for your brand or a local business · SSB × SST Hackathon 1 · Stockathon, a live trading simulation
In class
Financial Accounting · Marketing Management · Consumer Behaviour & Market Research · Macroeconomics & the Indian Economy · Organisational Behaviour & Teamwork · AI for Marketing & Customer Research
Immersions
Farm to brand (Akshayakalpa, a Coorg coffee estate, Mysore Silk)
Summer
Internship opportunity in a founder's office (8 weeks)

Year 2

Core Competency Development

Learn to manage core business functions and use data, technology and business strategy to grow an existing venture.

Term 3

August to December

You learn to manage a business through its busiest season.

Term project
Festive Season Launch, on your own online store and one marketplace
Beyond the classroom
AI Product 2, an AI Invoice & GST App used by 5 small businesses
In class
Managerial Accounting & Costing · Operations & Supply Chain Management · Sales & Distribution in India · Introduction to Product Management · Business Law, Ethics & Governance · AI for Finance & Operations
Immersions
Consumer goods manufacturing (Britannia, MTR Foods, Titan) · Winter: Singapore (Block71, Singapore Exchange, SMU)

Term 4

January to May

You learn to grow a business using content and social media.

Term project
Content-creation, through a channel for your own product or a niche you choose
Beyond the classroom
Live Project 1 with a national brand (e.g. Ajio) · SSB × SST Hackathon 2
In class
Corporate Finance · Business Strategy · Digital & Social Media Marketing · Data Analytics & Visualisation · Negotiation & Influence · AI for Content, Storytelling & Pitching
Immersions
Quick commerce and retail (Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, a Flipkart fulfilment centre)
Summer
Internship opportunity at a startup or company (8 weeks)

Year 3

Advanced Integration & Specialisation

Develop deep expertise in your chosen business specialisation and build a scalable venture, pitching to investors at VC Demo Day.

Specialisation tracks

  • Finance
  • Marketing & Branding
  • Tech, Data & Product
  • Strategy & Operations
  • Family Business (optional)

Term 5

August to December

You learn to start a venture in your chosen field.

Term project
HUSTLE Pre-Seed, with ₹50,000 in seed funding per team
Beyond the classroom
AI Product 3, an AI Sales Agent that books meetings for a real company · Live Project 2 (e.g. Practo) · SSB × SST Hackathon 3, your chance to find a tech co-founder
In class
New Venture Creation · Building AI Agents for Sales & Growth · 3 electives in your track · Career Practicum I (optional)
Immersions
Social impact (Akshaya Patra, SELCO Foundation, Hasiru Dala) · Winter: Dubai, China or Southeast Asia

Term 6

January to May

You learn to grow a venture and raise funding.

Term project
HUSTLE MVP and Investor Demo Day, where selected teams join Innovation Lab incubation
Beyond the classroom
Live Project 3 (e.g. Mokobara)
In class
Venture Finance & Fundraising · AI Strategy, Costs & Responsible Use · 3 electives in your track · Career Practicum II (optional)
Immersions
Family-run businesses (Surat textiles, TVS Motor, Himalaya Wellness)
Summer
Internship opportunity in your specialisation (8 to 10 weeks), or full-time work on your venture if incubated

Year 4

Industry Readiness

Apply your learnings through a capstone project in your specialisation and choose your path: startup, family business, or placements.

Term 7

August to December

You learn to solve real problems for an established company.

Term project
Industry Capstone, a live project with a partner company
Beyond the classroom
AI Product 4, a Hindi-English AI Voice Agent that makes 200+ calls for a real business
In class
Leadership & People Management · Building Voice & Multimodal AI Agents

Term 8

January to May

You step into your path: your startup, your family business or a job.

Build your startup
Selected founders continue at the Innovation Lab
Grow your family business
Lead an initiative in your family's business
Sit for campus placements
With startups and MNCs
